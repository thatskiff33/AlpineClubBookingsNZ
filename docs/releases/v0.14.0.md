# AlpineClubBookingsNZ v0.14.0

> Part of the [release notes index](README.md).

Release target: `v0.14.0` on 2026-11-17, a minor release, the first release after `v0.13.2`.

- Every change: the [`0.14.0 - 2026-11-17`](../../CHANGELOG.md#0140---2026-11-17) section of `CHANGELOG.md`.
- Upgrade steps: [`v0.13.2 → v0.14.0`](../UPGRADING.md#v0132--v0140) in the upgrade guide.
  Follow them in order around the deploy.

## What this release changes

This is a large release. It brings everything merged since `v0.13.2` into one
supported tag: 1,286 changelog entries, grouped by area in the changelog
section above. This page is the overview for a club's committee and its
operator. The changelog entries are the detailed record, and the upgrade guide
is the procedure.

Upgrading from `v0.13.2` needs an announced maintenance window, because the
release carries windowed migrations (the old release cannot keep running while
they apply). Read the [upgrade checklist](../UPGRADING.md#upgrade-checklist-for-this-release)
before you schedule the upgrade.

## What this release contains, by area

### Bookings

- Every change to a booking is now priced the moment it is saved, and none is
  sent to an officer to work out by hand (#4317).
- A member can find and add another club member as a guest, and everyone who
  ends up on somebody else's booking is told (#2306, #2307, #2308, #2309).
- Members can ask a Booking Officer to allow a booking that a booking policy
  would refuse, officers decide those requests in their own queue, and beds a
  forgotten request holds are given back automatically (#2524, #2526, #2553).
- A club can ask that an adult member stays alongside non-member guests
  (#2364).
- A school's booking now belongs to the school's own record, with no invented
  person standing in for it (#3369).
- An approved whole-lodge booking can be paid by card, with account credit or
  by internet banking (#4019).
- Members and lodge visitors can report a maintenance issue, and officers have
  a queue for them (#2780).
- Bookings awaiting review appear on the admin booking calendar (#4923), and
  the club chases bookings that still name a guest "Guest 1" before anyone
  arrives (#2550).

### Payments, refunds and credit

- Internet-banking refunds on a booking change, and approved refund appeals on
  internet-banking bookings, now ask the treasurer to send the money. The
  changelog calls each of these a behaviour change for every club (#3827).
- With the several-codes module on, a booking can carry several promo codes,
  and a booker can use their guests' codes (#3827, #3492). An officer can apply
  a member's missed promo code to a stay that has already finished (#4533).
- Card refunds are followed through: a refund the card provider fails later is
  owed again (#4104), refunds Stripe gave up on can be tried again or closed
  from Stuck States, including a group organiser's (#4368, #4851), and a refund
  that failed later can no longer be paid back twice (#4492).
- Card disputes (chargebacks) are recorded, emailed to Finance editors, and
  stop card refunds and account credit on that payment while open or lost.
- A Stripe payment the app did not create, and a booking card payment the app
  cannot match, now each send Finance editors an email instead of passing
  silently (#4532, #4624).
- A member whose account credit was used elsewhere in the club's accounts can
  be asked to pay for it. Raising those requests starts switched off.
- The officer money figures subtract refunds and credits and are renamed
  **Net Collected**, so the figures can move up or down compared with before
  (#3372).

### Membership

- A club can let a member with an unpaid subscription book at non-member rates
  instead of turning them away (#2543).
- A subscription billing category, such as Couple, can be set independently of
  a member's membership type (#4554).
- Family groups no longer record a rank against each member, and the old
  column is removed (#2520).
- A member who asked to be deleted can no longer be given their login back by
  the ordinary Reactivate action (#2620).
- Members can record dietary and allergy information on their profile, off
  until the club turns it on (#2941), and can write to a club message board,
  also off until switched on (#2994).

### Xero and accounting

- The Xero connection has its own table with no secret in it. Clubs upgrading
  from `v0.13.2` reconnect Xero once after the upgrade (#3806).
- Approving a manual credit grant raises its Xero credit note (#4431), and
  credit a treasurer uses in Xero is taken off the member's credit by itself
  (#4270).
- A payment deleted in Xero is retired in the app once Xero confirms it
  (#4531), and a credit note voided in Xero is now recorded.
- Members' dates of birth reach Xero (#2859), goodwill has its own Xero account
  (#2717), and the Xero Sync page warns when no Xero webhook has arrived for
  too long (#4432).
- The changelog records that the money journal is active, and that clubs use
  its default GST-inclusive 15% treatment until a tax settings screen is
  available (#4912).

### Privacy

- Each guest's dietary note on a booking is cleared automatically once the stay
  ended more than a set number of days ago. This ships switched on, with a
  30-day period (#4916).
- Download My Data covers every kind of personal record the club keeps, and
  Full Admins can produce the same file for anyone who asks (#4915).
- A guide for what to do after a privacy breach, a register of the outside
  companies that can receive members' data, and a "Where members' data is held"
  setting (#4918).
- The starter privacy policy and FAQ are corrected where a club has not edited
  them (#4031, #4914), and a club reviews personal-data retention before
  turning on automatic clean-up (#4457).
- Google Analytics is set up inside the app, and stops at this upgrade until an
  administrator re-enters the measurement id (#2573).

### Emails and notifications

- Club notices now reach every member who has not turned them off, while club
  newsletters still go only to members who opted in. Members who never saved
  their email preferences will now receive club notices (#4987).
- A bulk email asks you to confirm the audience and count before it sends
  (#4964).
- An officer can send a fresh copy of a booking confirmation, pre-arrival
  reminder or guest payment link from the booking's page.
- A test or staging copy of the site no longer emails the club's real members
  (#3035).

### Admin screens and the club website

- One admin menu per role: Admin, Bookings, Membership, Content, Finance and
  Treasurer. The menus are display only, and page access is unchanged.
- The admin search box finds every admin page you can open (#4797).
- The club's currency, number and date format (#3563) and time zone (#2989)
  are club settings rather than server settings.
- Website photo galleries show smaller copies of each photo instead of the
  full-size originals (#4935).
- Nothing on the public site advertises a booking a non-member can just make
  (#2430).
- A club can choose its conditions report and some booking timings (#4932).
- A new AI Diagnostics module, off by default, gives administrators a separate
  assistant with its own key and spend budget (#2371).

### Other lodges and the Alpine Central Server

- A club can connect to the Alpine Central Server and keep its other-lodges
  details in step automatically (#2949). Connecting is now one step.
- Members can share a message board post with every other club (#2992).
- Syncing pauses, and says so, when the central server runs a different
  software version, and resumes once the lagging side is upgraded.

### Operations and security

- Repeated wrong passwords on one account are slowed down (#4609), a new
  password found in a public breach list is refused (#4610), and
  password-reset and sign-in-link emails are limited per member (#4613).
- Every sign-in ends after a fixed time, 7 days by default (#4611). Members
  can use a passkey as their second sign-in step (#4617).
- Stored credentials and two-factor secrets can be protected by their own
  data-encryption key instead of the sign-in secret (#4456).
- A club can stop the booking system connecting to its database as the
  database superuser (#4453).
- The production deploy refuses a commit that is not a published release
  (#4978), and the site declares whether it is the club's live site or a copy
  (#3034, #4980).
- Database backups can be kept on the server as well as in Amazon S3, and
  restored from the admin screen.

## Before you upgrade

The [upgrade guide's section for this release](../UPGRADING.md#v0132--v0140)
opens with an ordered checklist: before the deploy, during the window and
after the deploy. Work through it in order. It points at each step's own
section and at the production runbook.

Several changelog entries also carry their own upgrade note. Read these
entries in the changelog section before you schedule the upgrade:

- card payments are now charged in the currency recorded on the Club Currency
  & Locale page (#3567);
- a booking-money census to run before deploying (#4317);
- Stripe webhook events to add once the new release alone serves (#4104, and
  the card disputes entry), and the Xero webhook's Credit Notes subscription
  (the voided credit note entry);
- the legacy finance route and extra hostnames, now off unless a club turns
  them on (#4981);
- the data-encryption key (#4456): until one is set, Admin → Setup shows the
  step as blocked on a club with stored provider credentials or
  authenticator-app secrets;
- one-off commands after the upgrade: edit reviews (#4343), payments deleted in
  Xero (#4531) and website photo copies (#4935);
- finishing the switch to the new version before processing a group
  organiser's cancellation (#3854).

## What members and officers will notice

- Anyone who signed in more than 7 days before the upgrade is asked to sign in
  again (#4611).
- Members who never saved their email preferences start receiving club
  notices (#4987).
- Stay dietary notes older than the clearing period are cleared on the first
  nightly run after the upgrade unless the club changes the setting first
  (#4916).
- Treasurers send internet-banking refunds on booking changes and approved
  appeals themselves (#3827).
- Officers see one admin menu per role, and Net Collected replaces the old
  revenue figures (#3372).
- Google Analytics stops until an administrator re-enters the measurement id
  (#2573), and Xero needs reconnecting once (#3806).

## Switched on, switched off, and not finished yet

- **On by default:** after-stay dietary clearing (#4916), the breached-password
  check (#4610) and the 7-day sign-in limit (#4611).
- **Off until a club turns it on:** AI Diagnostics (#2371), the message board
  (#2994), the member dietary field (#2941), several promo codes on one
  booking (#3826) and requests to pay for credit used elsewhere.
- **Groundwork that does nothing yet:** officers can be required to use an
  authenticator app or a passkey, and some actions can ask an officer to
  confirm who they are, but both stay off until a later release (#4619,
  #4994).

This release does not publish an npm package (`package.json` remains
private). The supported artefacts are the Git tag, the GitHub release and the
container images CI publishes to GHCR for the tagged commit.
