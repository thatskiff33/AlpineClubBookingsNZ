# The message board

Audience: Member

## What it is

A place to write something the club would want to know — a road report, a
reminder that the hut book came home, an offer of a lift up on Friday. You find
it at **Message board** (`/message-board`), and the newest few posts also appear
on your dashboard.

Everything you write here **stays inside your own club**. Nobody outside it can
see the board.

You will see a **Share with all clubs** tickbox under the writing box. It is
switched off and cannot be used yet — it is there so you know the option is
coming. Until it works, every post is club-only.

The board appears only if your club has switched the **Message board** module
on. If you cannot find it, that is why, and your club administrator can turn it
on.

## When you'd use it

- Something happened at the lodge that the next people up should know about.
- You want to reach members generally rather than emailing an officer.
- You are offering or looking for a lift, gear, or company on a trip.

The board is **not** the place for:

- Anything urgent or safety-critical — ring the person who needs to know.
- A maintenance fault. Use **Report a maintenance issue** so it reaches the
  people who fix things and is tracked until it is done.
- Booking a bed, changing a booking, or asking about money — those have their
  own screens and go to the right people.

## Step-by-step

### Read the board

1. Open **Message board** from your dashboard, or go to `/message-board`.
2. Posts are newest first. Each shows who wrote it and when.
3. **Older posts →** at the bottom moves back through the board. **← Back to
   the latest** returns you to the top.

### Write a post

1. Open **Message board**.
2. Type into the box at the top. You have up to 4000 characters — the counter
   under the box tells you how many you have used.
3. Click **Post**. It appears immediately, with your name on it.

Your name comes from your member record, so it is the name the club knows you
by. You cannot post under a different one.

### What you can write

Plain text. What you type is exactly what everybody else sees — if you type
something that looks like HTML or a web address, it appears as the characters
you typed rather than being turned into a link or hidden formatting.

Line breaks are kept, so you can lay a post out over a few lines.

## Good to know

- **Posts are limited to ten an hour.** That is far more than ordinary use; it
  is there so one person cannot fill the board.
- **You cannot edit or delete a post yourself.** Ask a club administrator, who
  can hide or remove it for you.
- **Your club administrator can moderate the board.** They can hide or remove
  any post.
- **A record is kept that you posted**, which you can see on your own account
  timeline. The record notes that you posted and how long the post was, not what
  it said.
