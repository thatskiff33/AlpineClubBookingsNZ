officer

## What to do

If the club's privacy notice lists what an AI app receives, the privacy officer
or an administrator updates it from the `ai-apps` row in
`docs/PRIVACY_RECIPIENTS.md`. The new categories are:

- email preferences;
- notices addressed to a committee role;
- account activity summaries, which may name other members;
- lodge roster names and nights;
- committee entries, including free-text descriptions;
- two-step sign-in status.

AI connections stay off unless switched on in Admin → System → AI connections.

## How to check it worked

The published notice lists each category, or the club confirms AI connections
are not switched on.
