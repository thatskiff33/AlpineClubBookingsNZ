- **Download invoice when the club does not use Xero (#5066).** With the club's Xero module off, the booking page's **Download invoice** button saves an invoice the app makes from the booking, and the **Invoice and credits** card shows the same figures. The invoice is titled **Invoice** and comes from the club's legal name and postal address, with the lodge named as the place of stay; it shows the download date and booking reference (no invoice number), one line per guest for each run of nights at one price, one line per promo code, any change fee, the total, then account credit applied, paid less refunds and credits returned, anything kept under the booking's change terms, and what is still to pay. It carries no GST wording and no bank details. A booking awaiting the club's review gets one marked **Not yet approved**. The booker, admins and booking officers can download it; there is none for a cancelled, draft or waitlisted booking, a place the group organiser pays for, or a night still awaiting a price, and the page asks the member to contact the club instead for a booking that already has an invoice in the club's accounts, one with an extra charge raised (even if withdrawn), or one made in a period the club recorded as GST-registered (for a group place, when its organiser's booking was made) until the "Tax invoice" form is built (#5291). A single statement PDF for a Xero club's booking is #5292. Each download is recorded in the audit log under Booking. **Club identity** gains **Legal name** (defaults to the club name) and **Postal address** fields for it. Nothing changes for a club with Xero on. Operators: one additive migration runs on the next `migrate deploy`.
