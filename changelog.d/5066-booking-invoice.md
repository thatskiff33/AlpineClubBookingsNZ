- **Download invoice on the booking page (fork #115).** Beside **Edit Booking**, the booker and officers can download a PDF invoice. A booking with a Xero invoice gets Xero's own; otherwise the app generates one from the booking — guests, nights, discounts, the total, and the amount paid and balance due once anything is paid — with a blank invoice number. It is for clubs not using Xero, and for organisations that need an invoice before a group booking is approved. A guest on someone else's booking does not see it; a cancelled booking and a place paid by a group organiser get no invoice. **Club Identity → Lodge details**, and each lodge's **Edit** form under **Setup → Lodges** (fork #123), gain optional **Postal address**, **Bank details for bookings** (account name and a New Zealand account number, entered in four parts) and **GST number** fields, printed on the generated invoice. Operators: one additive migration runs on the next `migrate deploy`.
