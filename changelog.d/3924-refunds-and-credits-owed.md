- **Net Collected now takes off a refund still owed on a live booking straight away** (#3372, the owner's decision of 7 Oct 2026). When a paid booking is edited down and the refund is to be paid back by hand, or an approved refund request is to be paid back by hand, Net Collected used to keep counting that money until the treasurer marked it paid back. It now comes off as soon as it is owed, as a cancelled booking's already did: $200.00 paid and edited down by $50.00 shows $150.00. The dashboard card's breakdown line now says "owed back by hand" rather than "owed back on cancellation".
- **Card refunds Stripe has not yet paid, and late card charges waiting for the treasurer, now come off Net Collected too** (#3372, the owner's decisions of 7 Oct 2026). A card refund the club has started that is still pending, processing, or failed and waiting for a retry now comes off straight away, and so does a late card charge on a cancelled booking until the treasurer decides to keep it. On a cancelled booking, a bank transfer that arrived after the cancel and was credited to the member's account no longer counts as kept either. The dashboard card's breakdown line names each of these when it is not zero.
- **"Refunds owed" and "Credits owed" now sit beside every Net Collected figure** (#3372, the owner's request of 7 Oct 2026): on the dashboard card, the Payments page, Reports (and its CSV) and the Finance dashboard. **Refunds owed** is every refund owed and not yet paid: refunds promised back by hand, card refunds Stripe has not yet paid, and late card charges waiting for the treasurer's refund-or-keep decision. **Credits owed** is all the account credit members hold and have not yet used. Both are as at today and across the whole club, so the page's dates, lodge and filters do not change them, and each amount comes off Net Collected only for its own booking. A refund leaves the figure when it is paid, dismissed or kept, and credit leaves it when it is spent on a booking. On a phone, the two figures stack under the dashboard card's title.
