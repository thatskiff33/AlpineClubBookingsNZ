- **A cancelled or bumped stay now tells the member to take it out of their calendar (#5249).**
  The Booking Confirmed email lets a member add their stay to their calendar. Until now, if the booking was later cancelled or bumped, that calendar entry stayed and nothing told the member. The Booking Cancelled and Booking Update (bumped) emails now attach a calendar file that cancels the same entry, with one sentence asking the member to remove the stay if it is still showing. When a booking's dates change, the Booking Modified email says the calendar entry still shows the old dates; no file is attached there.

  Only members who were sent the confirmation, and are still allowed its booking link, get the file and the sentence. Calendar apps differ in how they treat such a file, and an entry added through the Google Calendar or Outlook.com link cannot be removed by any file, so the sentence always asks the member to check. If your club saved its own wording for these emails, it is kept: the file is still attached, and you can add `{{calendarRemovalNote}}` (cancelled and bumped) or `{{calendarUpdateNote}}` (modified) to show the sentence. See [the email messages guide](../docs/guides/email-messages.md).

  Retried cancellation emails also keep the standard calendar sentence consistent with whether the attachment could be rebuilt.
