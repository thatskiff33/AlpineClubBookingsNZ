- **Unreadable school teacher details are visible to booking officers (#3485).**
  A school request whose saved teacher names or email cannot be read now shows
  a specific attention warning instead of silently displaying no teachers.
  Quoting, holding and approval are disabled on that card; the officer can
  check with the requester and decline it for resubmission, or ask support to
  repair the stored row. Healthy requests and general requests are unchanged.
