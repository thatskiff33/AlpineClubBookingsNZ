- **Other lodges: each season now has an end date as well as a start ([triyder/AlpineClubBookingsNZ#75](https://github.com/triyder/AlpineClubBookingsNZ/issues/75)).** On **Admin → Lodges → Other lodges**, the editor has **Winter season start date**, **Winter season end date**, **Summer season start date** and **Summer season end date**. The end date is the season's last day. Seasons repeat every year (only the day and month count) and may cross New Year, for example summer 1 December to 31 March. A season with no end date yet runs until the other season starts, as before. The two seasons may not share a day, and **Save** says what to change if they do. The dates are shared with the Alpine Central Server, which now prices each night of a stay on its public lodge search at that night's season and shows "Price not available for these dates" when any night of a stay is outside both seasons. A season ending on 28 February also covers 29 February in a leap year. This site and the central server must both be on **2.6**; syncing pauses while they differ. The database change only adds two optional columns and needs no maintenance window.
