- **Other lodges: your own lodge's non-member rates and season dates can come from the Fees page ([triyder/AlpineClubBookingsNZ#77](https://github.com/triyder/AlpineClubBookingsNZ/issues/77)).** On **Admin → Lodges → Other lodges**, the rate age groups are now **Infant**, **Child**, **Youth** and **Adult**, the built-in names of the club's age tiers, so each club fills a group from its own tier (whatever ages it covers). Your own lodge's edit popup has a new switch, **Rates and season dates from the Fees page**, which is **on** by default. While it is on, the eight non-member rates are the **Non-Member** membership type's nightly rates on **Admin → Fees** (each age group takes the age tier of that built-in name, or the type's single rate when it has no age groups), and each season's start and end are those of the lodge's active season of that type that is running today, or else the next one to start, or else the one that ended last. They are shown read-only in the popup and refreshed when a season is changed on the Fees or Seasons page, when the lodge is saved, and before each upload to the Alpine Central Server, so other clubs see the new rates on the next upload. When the Fees page has no Non-Member rate, or the lodge is not linked to one of this site's lodges, nothing is changed and the popup says why. Each change is recorded in the audit log with the values before and after (`OTHER_LODGE_RATES_FROM_FEES`, category `lodge`). **On upgrading, the rates and dates you typed are replaced from the Fees page at the next upload: check the Non-Member rates on Admin → Fees first, or turn the switch off.** Turn the switch off to type the rates and dates yourself. The switch stays on this site and is not shared. The database change only adds one column (default on) and needs no maintenance window.
