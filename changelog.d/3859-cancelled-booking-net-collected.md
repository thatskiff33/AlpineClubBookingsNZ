- **Net Collected now counts only money a cancelled booking actually left with the club** (#3372, the owner's review on #3811). A cancelled booking that was never paid adds nothing; one paid and fully refunded adds nothing; one where the club kept a policy fee adds that fee. Account credit the club kept on a cancelled booking now counts, and a refund still owed back by an officer on a cancelled booking is taken off straight away. Refunds are now worked out per payment, so a refund recorded against a payment that never took money can no longer reduce another booking's figure. The card is renamed from "Net Collected Cash" to **Net Collected** on Payments, Reports and Finance, with the same breakdown line underneath.
