- **A booking with the whole lodge no longer has the custodian's bed as well
  (#2698).** When a group takes the lodge exclusively, they now have every bed
  except one a hut leader is holding for those nights. Before this, the same bed
  was counted as both theirs and the custodian's — the club's own documentation
  said the custodian's bed sat outside the held pool, and the system did the
  opposite.

  Nothing a member sees changes. The lodge still reads as full on a held night,
  no new beds become bookable, and no other booking can be admitted onto those
  nights. **What the group pays is unchanged too**: a whole-lodge night is
  charged at a flat rate for the lodge and never by the bed, so a narrower set
  of beds costs exactly the same.

  **Where an officer will notice it** is the Hut Leaders page. If you hold a bed
  for a hut leader on nights a booking has already taken the whole lodge, the
  page now stops and asks you first: it lists the nights, and you either accept
  — in which case the assignment is created and that booking gives up the one
  bed on those nights, as a single recorded action — or cancel, in which case
  nothing changes at all, on either side. Their dates, their price and every
  other booking are untouched.

  It only asks in that direction. Putting a whole-lodge hold on a booking over
  nights a custodian already holds needs no question and raises none, because
  the custodian's bed was never part of what the hold covers. No existing
  assignment or booking is rewritten: the rule applies from the moment a bed
  hold is created or changed.

  Three smaller changes come with it. Creating, changing and deleting a
  hut-leader assignment are now recorded in the Audit Log under **Lodge**, and
  an accepted amendment is recorded under **Booking** so it can be found from
  either side. Deleting an assignment now takes the same lodge lock the other
  two already did, because handing a bed back is a capacity change like any
  other. And the Bed Allocation board's picture of a held lodge no longer shows
  the custodian's bed as belonging to the holding group — it shows it as the
  custodian's, which is who is actually asleep in it.
