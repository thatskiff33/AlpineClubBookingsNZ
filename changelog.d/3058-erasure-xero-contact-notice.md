- **When a member is erased, you can now see whether they left a Xero customer
  behind (#3058).** Erasing a member here removes their details from this
  application. It does not ask Xero to change, archive or delete their contact —
  that has always been the intention, because Xero is the club's accounting
  system and it is administered separately. What was missing is that nobody was
  ever told. The Xero contact went on existing, nothing here pointed at it any
  more, and there was no way to find out short of going looking.

  **Admin → Finance → Xero Sync → Erased members with a Xero contact** is that
  telling. Each line is one Xero contact an erasure left behind, oldest first,
  with a link straight to that contact in Xero and a note of which kind of
  erasure it was. Nothing on the screen changes anything in Xero.

  Whether such a contact should be archived, merged, edited or left exactly as
  it is remains entirely the decision of whoever administers Xero. This
  application is not asking for anything to be removed, and invoices and
  accounting history raised against a contact stay valid and usable either way.

  When you have archived some of them in Xero, **Check these in Xero** asks Xero
  about the contacts on the list and drops the ones you have dealt with. It is
  the only thing that makes the list shrink: Contact Sync never fetches archived
  contacts, so on its own it would never notice. A contact nobody deals with
  stays listed indefinitely, which is right — there really is a customer in Xero
  that nothing here points at.

  Nothing on the screen names the erased person, and the check does not bring
  their details back — it keeps the contact's status and nothing else. A line
  carries an id and a link, because their details are exactly what the erasure
  removed; they are still in Xero, where they are yours to read.

  One thing erasure does still do in Xero, and it always has: it cancels the
  member's future bookings, and cancelling one they had paid for raises a credit
  note, exactly as any other cancellation would.

  Only an officer with finance access can see the list.
