- **When a member is erased, you can now see whether they left a Xero customer
  behind (#3058).** Erasing a member here removes their details from this
  application and does nothing at all in Xero — that has always been the
  intention, because Xero is the club's accounting system and it is administered
  separately. What was missing is that nobody was ever told. The Xero contact
  went on existing, nothing here pointed at it any more, and there was no way to
  find out short of going looking.

  **Admin → Finance → Xero Sync → Erased members with a Xero contact** is that
  telling. It is a notice, not a tool: there is nothing on it to press, and
  nothing it does can change Xero or this application. Each line is one Xero
  contact an erasure left behind, oldest first, with a link straight to that
  contact in Xero and a note of which kind of erasure it was.

  Whether such a contact should be archived, merged, edited or left exactly as
  it is remains entirely the decision of whoever administers Xero. This
  application is not asking for anything to be removed, and invoices and
  accounting history raised against a contact stay valid and usable either way.
  If you do archive one in Xero, the line drops off the list the next time
  Contact Sync runs.

  Nothing on the screen names the erased person. A line carries an id and a
  link, because their details are exactly what the erasure removed — they are
  still in Xero, where they are yours to read.

  Only an officer with finance access can see the list.
