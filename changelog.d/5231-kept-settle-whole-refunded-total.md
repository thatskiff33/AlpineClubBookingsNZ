- **A cancel made just as a bank transfer is matched no longer credits the invoice correction as cash (#4449).** When Xero's record of a paid invoice could not say exactly how much cash came in, the app recorded the bank transfer at the booking's stored price and counted the invoice correction notes against it a moment later. A member cancelling in that moment could be given account credit for the corrected amount as if they had paid it. The payment's refunded total is now worked out in full when the transfer is matched, and the credit-note sync works it out again under the same lock, so a cancel straight afterwards sees the net amount the member actually paid. Nothing changes for operators.
