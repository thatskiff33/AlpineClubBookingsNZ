- **Credit used elsewhere is now asked for straight away, even while a booking
  change is still unpaid.** When account credit applied to a booking turns out to
  have been used elsewhere in the club's accounts while the member still owes for a
  booking change, the two are now one payment request for the combined amount,
  emailed at once and worded as both, instead of the credit waiting until the
  change was paid. The member pays once by card, and the app records that payment
  in Xero against both invoices: the credit on the booking's own invoice, the change
  on its own invoice. Nothing is recorded twice, and nobody has to fix anything in
  Xero by hand. Where the change's Xero invoice cannot follow the merged request
  (for example it has already been sent), the credit still waits for the change to
  be paid or withdrawn, as before, and the officers are told why.
