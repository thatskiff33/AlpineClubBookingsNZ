### Added

- Finance officers can sync a member, booking or the scheduled whole-club inbound cycle from Xero now, with permission gates, rate limiting, audit history and result summaries.
- Scheduled and manual inbound cycles share a PostgreSQL run claim that releases when the holder exits.
- Xero Sync shows the last signed webhook received and a configurable silence warning (24 hours by default), also surfaced in Needs Attention and Stuck States.
