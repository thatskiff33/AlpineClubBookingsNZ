- **The starter privacy policy now lists every outside service that can
  receive members' information, and names your host from your own settings
  (#5007).** Section 5 used to name a fixed host and region that were not true
  for every club. It now lists payments, accounting, email, hosting, backups,
  Google, AI help, error reporting, breached-password checks, address lookup,
  video meetings and the Alpine Central Server, links to the privacy notice
  the register records for each, and marks the optional ones "Only if the
  club turns it on". The host and the place your data is held come from
  **Where members' data is held** under Club Identity; until that is filled
  in, the page tells members to ask your Privacy Officer. Backups move into section 5's list, and section 6
  refers to the host described there.

  A privacy page that still holds the unedited starter text is updated once on
  upgrade. A page your club edited is not changed: check its sections 5 and 6
  yourself. Importing a configuration bundle exported before this release no
  longer brings the replaced starter text back. Upgrade to the release that
  added the hosting setting first.
