- **An edit that lowers the price of a disputed card booking no longer refunds
  or credits the member.** While the member's card payment is disputed (open or
  lost), changing the dates, removing a guest or a batch edit records the change
  and hands the whole reduction to the treasurer as one task naming the dispute,
  as a cancel already did. The edit screen and the Booking Modified email say
  the refund is with the treasurer, and an officer review can no longer choose
  account credit for a disputed payment. Finance editors also get one more
  email if a dispute reported lost is later reported won, so the Xero loss
  entry can be reversed; `DEPLOYMENT.md` describes the steps.
