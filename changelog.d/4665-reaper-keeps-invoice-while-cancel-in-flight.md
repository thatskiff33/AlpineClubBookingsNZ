- **A changed booking's Xero invoice is no longer thrown away while Stripe is slow to cancel the old payment request (#4665).** When a member's booking change was replaced by a newer payment request, and Stripe had not yet confirmed the cancel of the old request (an outage, or a retry waiting its turn), the nightly tidy-up of old waiting invoices could cancel the old change's Xero invoice instead of moving it to the new request. When the member then paid, no invoice named that change, the booking's main invoice could be overpaid, and the booking-vs-Xero check reported a missing invoice. The tidy-up now leaves that invoice waiting until the cancel finishes, and the cancel's last step moves it to the new request, so the member's payment is invoiced for both changes, each for its own amount. A cancel that has given up for good is handled as before.
