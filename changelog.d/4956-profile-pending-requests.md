- **The profile now remembers a pending account-deletion request or email
  change.** A member who has asked for their account to be deleted sees the
  date they asked, in place of the Request Account Deletion button, until an
  admin approves or rejects it — even after a reload. A member waiting to click
  an email-change link sees the new address and when the link expires in the
  Change Email section. Before, both vanished on reload, and the only way to
  check a deletion request was to go through the irreversible-action dialog
  again (#4956).
