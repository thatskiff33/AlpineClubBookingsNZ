- **A saved-card payment that arrives after the booking was paid another way is now always reported (#5240).**
  If a saved-card charge went through but the app stopped before marking the booking paid, and the booking was then paid another way (account credit, a $0 total, or the group organiser), the card payment that arrived later used to be kept with nobody told. The administrators now get the same "Kept card payment" alert as for any other card payment kept on a paid booking, at most once a day per payment. If the card payment is still not matched to its booking, after an hour the payments task sends that alert itself; if the booking is still unpaid a week later, the case is written to the error log for someone to check by hand.
  When a duplicate card payment is refunded by the background retry rather than at the first attempt, the booking history now names the payment that settled the booking, as it already did for a refund made straight away. No money moves differently.
