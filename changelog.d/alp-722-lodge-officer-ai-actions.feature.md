- **Officers with Lodge Operations can now use a connected AI app for that area's admin work (ALP-722).**
  50 new actions cover hut leaders, chore rosters and chores, work parties, lodges and lodge settings, the Other lodges registry, maintenance reports and their questions and policy, the QR signs' on/off switch, reading the food-order settings, and creating, changing and deleting club calendar events. Each is offered only to an officer who holds Lodge Operations (view for reads, edit for changes), on a connection they approved on the officer screen, and the route still checks the officer's permissions as for the browser. Every change is marked destructive, so the app asks the officer first. The kiosk's sign-in account, a hut leader's kiosk PIN and the display screens' setup stay with the kiosk; the lodge instructions, minting a maintenance QR sign and a meeting's host link stay on the website; so do saving how much of each name a lodge's member roster shows (an app may read it), the printable chore roster, pricing or receiving a food order, saving the food-order settings and changing the club's booking officer contact details in the Other lodges registry. A connection is never sent a door code, kiosk PIN, QR sign link, report photo or the food officer's email address. Editing or deleting a calendar event now refuses a `scope` other than single or series instead of treating it as one occurrence. Nothing changes until a club switches on **Officers may connect an AI app**.
