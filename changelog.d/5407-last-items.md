- **The Xero sync error alert's one-an-hour limit now holds across copies of the app (#5407).**
  The alert still goes out at most once an hour across every kind of Xero error. It now keeps its own record of each alert raised instead of reading the mail log, so three things an operator may notice: two copies of the app hitting a Xero error at the same moment now send one alert, not two; an alert left in the mail log for the retry job, or withheld, counts as sent for the hour, as one already sent did; and while the alert is switched off, or no officer is opted in to it, it is tried at most once a day, so after it is switched back on or an officer opts in, the next alert can wait up to a day after the last skipped attempt. Just after this release, one extra alert can go out within the hour of an earlier one. Alerts about single payments or cancellations that are sent straight to officers are unchanged and not limited by this.
