- **Two-factor set-up shows a QR code, and Enter submits the code.** Setting up
  an authenticator app now shows a QR code to scan, drawn in the browser so the
  secret never leaves the page, with the setup key still shown underneath for
  typing. The App, Email and Recovery choices on the set-up and sign-in screens
  now say which one is selected, in words and to screen readers, rather than by
  shading alone. Pressing Enter in a code box enrols or verifies, as clicking
  the button does.
