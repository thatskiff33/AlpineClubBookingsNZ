- **Administrators can now moderate the club message board (#2998).** A new
  **Admin → Members → Message Board** screen lists everything members have
  posted, with search by wording or author, and a tab for anything currently
  hidden.

  **Hide** takes a post off the member board and the dashboard card straight
  away and is reversible — the text is kept and **Show to members** puts it
  back. **Edit text** corrects a post, writing what it replaced to the audit log
  so the member's original wording can still be recovered. **Remove** deletes
  the text permanently and cannot be undone.

  This is what makes the board safe to leave switched on: until now a member
  could post and nobody could take it down. Members still cannot edit or delete
  their own posts, so those requests come to you.

  Hiding, editing and removing are membership-area edit actions — a view-only
  membership administrator can read the board but not change it, and every
  action is recorded in the audit log against the administrator who took it.
