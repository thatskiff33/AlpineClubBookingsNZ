- **A bank-transfer payment's refunded total no longer goes stale or wrong after Xero changes (#5289).**
  Five gaps left after #4449 are closed. A price correction later voided or deleted in Xero is now taken back off the payment, even when the app's first look at the note is the voided one, so a later cancel gives back the right amount. The app's own allocation of an edit's credit note, and a payment found only through its invoice link, now wait for a bank-transfer settle running at the same time, so a cancel straight after reads the right figure. A $100 correction beside a $200 bank refund whose credit note is still waiting to go to Xero no longer drops the total to $200 or tells the officer a credit note may be missing. A card payment's refunded total is never lowered by Xero again: it is kept, logged and alerted, as before #4449. And the alert a bank-transfer settle owes when it keeps the club's recorded refunds is now recorded with the change, so it is sent exactly once even if the app stops part-way, and it stays owed until it reaches an admin or waits in the email retry; the hourly limit on other Xero alerts no longer swallows it. The booking's staff timeline shows it as "Bank-transfer refunds kept above what Xero shows", and screens that read a booking's cancellation figures skip that entry. The booking repair tool no longer switches back on an allocation Xero removed, even one removed while the tool runs; it leaves it for an officer to review. A credit-note reading from Xero that carries no status no longer turns the note's allocations off. No database change. A payment already left wrong by a voided correction is corrected the next time that note syncs.
