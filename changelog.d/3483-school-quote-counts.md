- **A school quote is now priced for the numbers you typed, not the numbers the
  school first sent (#3412).** When a school group changed size after
  submitting — agreed down from 29 people to 20, say — the officer panel showed
  the new total but **Save quote** priced and emailed the old one. The adjusted
  numbers were only applied at **Approve & invoice school**, so every quote the
  school received, and every bed reserved when the quote was sent, was for the
  group as originally submitted.

  Saving the quote is now the moment the change takes effect. The adjusted
  numbers are applied to the request itself, and the price, the list of
  attendees the school sees, the guest count in their email, the beds held when
  the quote is sent, and the booking created at approval all come from that one
  list. The named teachers and parent helpers are kept as they are; only the
  child numbers change, and the group still cannot exceed the lodge's capacity.

  Two things you will notice. If beds are already held for this request, the new
  numbers cannot be saved until you press **Release hold** — the held beds are
  not silently re-sized, and the panel now says so before you click. And if a
  club member is linked to one of the school's unnamed children, changing the
  numbers is refused rather than quietly moving that member onto a different
  child: unlink them first. Changing the teachers or parent helpers is still not
  done here.
