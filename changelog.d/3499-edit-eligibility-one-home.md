- **The rule for which bookings can still be changed now lives in one place
  (#3245).** Four different screens and services let a booking be edited — a
  guest added, a guest taken off, the dates moved, or several things at once —
  and each of them has to decide first whether the booking is at a stage where
  changing it is allowed at all.

  Three of the four carried their own copy of that list, written out by hand.
  The copies happened to agree, so nothing was wrong for a club today. The
  problem was what came next: changing the rule — deciding, say, that a
  finished stay may still have a guest added afterwards — meant changing it in
  four places, and would almost certainly have been changed in three. An
  earlier fault in this same area was caused by exactly that, a list copied
  from a neighbouring screen rather than from the real rule.

  The three copies are gone. All four now ask the same rule, and the message a
  member sees when a booking is too far along to change is written from that
  rule rather than typed out beside it, so the two can never say different
  things. Nothing about which bookings may be edited has changed.

  One small improvement comes with it: when an administrator uses the override
  that lets them move the dates of a booking that has already finished, and it
  is refused for some other reason, the refusal now names the right set of
  stages. It used to quote the ordinary list, which did not include the very
  stage the override exists to reach.
