- **The booking page and the payment link now say which lodge a booking is at
  (#4948).** In a club with more than one lodge, a member could open a booking
  or an emailed payment link and pay without anything on the screen saying
  which lodge the stay was at, even though a booking at the wrong lodge has to
  be moved or cancelled afterwards. The booking page's Stay Details card now
  starts with the lodge, the payment link's card shows it above the dates, and
  the arrival card is titled "How to get to" the booking's own lodge rather
  than the club's default one. These always show, in a one-lodge club too, as
  the booking form's review step already does.

  My Bookings and the dashboard's Next Stay, draft and Recent Bookings rows now
  show each booking's lodge under its dates when the club has more than one
  active lodge, and on any booking still at a lodge that has been archived. A
  club with one lodge sees these lists exactly as before.
