- **A retried extra card payment is recorded in Xero even when a credit release already paid the same invoice (#4435).**
  After a crash, the receipt used to treat any live payment with the same Stripe reference as its own. A credit release on the booking's main invoice uses that same reference, so the retry could adopt the release's payment and record nothing. It now accepts a payment only when the reference matches, the amount equals the receipt, and that payment is not already the credit release's. Otherwise it records its own payment. The money was already collected; both payments now show in Xero.
