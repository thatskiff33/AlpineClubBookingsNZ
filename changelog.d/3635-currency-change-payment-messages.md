- **A payment still being processed is no longer shown as paid, and a group
  settlement can no longer be charged twice while an earlier payment is still
  being processed (#3635).** On the booking page's "Additional Payment
  Required" card, a payment the provider is still processing now says it is
  being processed, and a card payment refused because of the club's currency
  now shows as an error beside the amount still owing, instead of both
  appearing as a green "paid" message. When a group organiser's earlier
  settlement payment is still being processed (a bank debit, say, or one started
  before a currency change), the organiser is now told to wait rather than being
  offered a second payment beside it, even if the group's total has changed
  since.
