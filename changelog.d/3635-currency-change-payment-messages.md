- **A payment still being processed is no longer shown as paid, and a group
  settlement can no longer be charged twice after a currency change (#3635).**
  On the booking page's "Additional Payment Required" card, a payment the
  provider is still processing now says it is being processed, and a card
  payment refused because of the club's currency now shows as an error beside
  the amount still owing, instead of both appearing as a green "paid" message.
  When the club's currency has changed and a group settlement payment started in
  the old currency is still being processed, the organiser is now told to wait
  rather than being offered a second payment beside it.
