- **A failed Xero action now moves you to its explanation, every time (#4975).** On **Admin → Finance → Xero** and **Xero member grouping**, a failed retry, replay, sync, contact creation, link repair, family-group creation, mode or rule change, or disconnect now shows its message where you can see it and moves the screen and keyboard focus to it. Pressing the same button again and getting the same failure brings you back to the message. This also works while the card holding the button is collapsed. A list or status that could not be loaded in the background still shows a quiet notice and leaves focus where it was. Read-only checks behave the same way: the missing-contacts dry run, the duplicate scan and **Refresh from Xero**.
