- **A booking edit no longer goes to a person because of a guest it does not
  touch (#3531, stage 1 of 3).** When a guest's nightly prices came from an
  import that split their total evenly across the nights, any edit to the
  booking — even one that added or removed a different guest — was parked for
  an officer to price, because the untouched guest's nights could not be
  vouched for one by one. Their money never moved; their rows were kept exactly
  as they were. The edit now judges an untouched guest by their total, as it
  already did a guest being removed whole, and prices exactly. A guest whose
  own nights the edit moves is still judged night by night and still parks when
  those nights were never priced individually — that is the case a person
  genuinely has to settle. The same reading applies to the itemised Xero lines:
  a night the edit keeps needs no per-night proof, so a removal beside such a
  guest now itemises where it fell back.
