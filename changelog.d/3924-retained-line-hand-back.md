- **A cancelled booking's "Non-refundable amount retained" no longer counts a refund the club still owes** (#3372, the owner's review on #3811). On the booking page's Cancellation Outcome, a cancelled booking paid by hand whose refund is still an open task for an officer used to show the whole unrefunded payment as retained. The refund owed now comes off straight away, as it already does in Net Collected, and the line says so: for example "$30.00 (after $60.00 still being paid back by hand)". The line now always matches what Net Collected counts in cash for that booking. The "retained amount" in the booking page's messages follows the same figure. A card refund Stripe has not yet paid, a late card charge waiting for the treasurer, and a late bank transfer credited to the member come off the line the same way. Bookings with nothing still owed back show exactly what they did before.
