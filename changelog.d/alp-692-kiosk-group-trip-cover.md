- **The lodge kiosk shows adult cover from another booking in a Group Trip
  again, instead of always saying it needs re-checking (#5239).** At a club
  that lets an adult in one booking cover the children in another booking of
  the same trip, a hut leader now sees the real figures on those cards — for
  example *1 of 2 nights covered*, from *another booking in the same Group
  Trip* — once the kiosk can confirm the answer is current.

  It still says *needs re-checking* whenever it cannot: while a change to
  another booking in the trip is waiting to be checked again, at a lodge whose
  adult cover rule sends bookings to a Booking Officer rather than stopping
  them, after the Group Trip cover option has been switched off, for a very
  large trip, and for a stay that is already over. Bookings last checked
  before this release keep saying *needs re-checking* until they are next
  checked. See [the lodge guide](../docs/guides/lodge.md#group-trips-on-the-day-list).
