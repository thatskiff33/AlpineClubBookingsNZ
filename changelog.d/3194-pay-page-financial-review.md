- **The payment link a member is emailed no longer says their booking is
  settled while the club is still working out what it costs (#3194).** When a
  change to a booking saves but the refund or charge for it cannot be read from
  the booking's stored price history, the money waits for the office. The
  booking's own page has said so since #3033. The `/pay/...` link did not: it
  showed the ordinary payment view, and on a booking that read as paid it told
  the member there was nothing more to do. One member, one booking, two answers
  — and the reassuring one was the one that had not checked.

  That link now says the same thing the booking page says, in the same words,
  because both take those sentences from one file. A booking whose money is
  being worked out is told the club is doing so, that nothing has been refunded
  or charged for it yet, and that there is nothing they need to do about that
  change — and never a figure for that change, because the figure is the thing
  nobody knows yet.

  **A member who has already paid is still told their payment arrived.** Their
  page keeps *"we've received your payment of $360.00 on 12 Aug — your stay is
  confirmed"*, and the review sentences are added underneath it; only *"nothing
  more to do"* goes. Without that, the one page whose whole purpose is a payment
  had nothing at all to say about it — which is the same defect this entry is
  about, wearing different words. The amount kept is money the club has, not the
  amount being worked out, and the wording says in as many words that the two are
  different.

  **The member can still pay, and every way to pay stays open.** A change can
  give back nights that cannot be priced while adding nights that price
  normally, so the booking's own stay is still going ahead and this link is the
  only way an unregistered guest can pay for it. The card button, the internet
  banking reference and the "email me a new link" button all remain, with the
  review note sitting directly under the amount so it is read before the member
  decides. Taking the payment away would have cost them the booking when the
  hold expired without moving a cent of the money in question. The note stays on
  screen after they pay, too, so the last thing the page says is not "your
  booking is confirmed, see you at the lodge".

  **And the amount on that page says out loud that it is out of date.** When a
  change is parked, the new dates are saved and a departing guest's row is
  deleted, but the booking's total is deliberately left exactly as it was —
  changing it would mean knowing by how much, which is the question under
  review. So the payment card shows the new stay beside the old price, and it
  looks completely settled. A member who reads it that way and pays it hands
  over more than they may owe, by precisely the amount nobody could work out.
  The note now opens with *"This amount does not yet reflect the change you made
  to this booking"* wherever the figure is one the member is being asked to pay.
  Beside a payment that has already arrived it keeps the old sentence, because
  money the club has received is a settled fact and nothing about the change can
  make it out of date.

  **If they do pay by card, the money can come back to that card.** It could
  not before, and that was the trap underneath the reassurance. When a review is
  raised, the club records which payment the money would come out of — and if
  the booking had not been paid yet, it records nothing, for ever. So the
  ordinary sequence "the change is parked, then the member pays" ended with a
  review that had no card behind it, and the officer working it could only ever
  hand the money back as club credit: not to the card it came from, and not
  spendable anywhere but this club. The club now looks at where the booking's
  money actually is at the moment the officer closes the review, rather than at
  a note taken before the member had paid, so a card refund goes back to the
  card and a bank transfer is recorded as a bank transfer. A booking that
  genuinely has no money behind it still becomes account credit, exactly as
  before, and every existing limit — the cap on what a card can give back, the
  refusals that leave the task open for someone to look at — is reached the same
  way it always was.

  A booking with no review waiting is unchanged in every respect.
