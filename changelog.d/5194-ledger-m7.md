- **Subscription charges and joining fees now post into the member-money journal
  (Ledger M7, #4519).** Confirming a subscription bill posts its charge, one
  revenue line per component, owed by the invoice's recipient. Queuing a
  joining-fee invoice posts that fee once per member, or an explicit zero for a
  member the club exempts. A manual mark-paid or a close as already paid posts
  the cash against the charge, and a mark-unpaid takes it back. A cancellation
  credit note for the whole of an unpaid charge reverses that charge. When a
  voided, unpaid subscription invoice is billed again, the new bill replaces the
  old debt so the member owes it once; a void on its own posts nothing. Nothing
  reads the journal yet, so no screen, email, invoice or Xero document changes.
  Charges and fees from before this release are not posted; a later step
  back-posts their history.
