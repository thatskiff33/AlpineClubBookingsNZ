- **A group's combined Xero invoice is now checked against each joiner's ledger charges too.**
  When an organiser settles a group by internet banking, the app now records whether the one combined invoice sent to Xero bills exactly what the ledger says each joiner's booking charged, or why it does not, and makes sure a joiner's charge is never recorded as billed by both that invoice and the joiner's own invoice. When the combined invoice is voided, or Xero no longer has it, that record is closed so the group's replacement invoice can take over. What is sent to Xero, emailed or voided, and when, is unchanged. Clubs need to do nothing. A new 15-minute job, "Combined group invoice cover release" (`combined-invoice-cover-release`), appears on the admin cron health page; it only closes those records and changes nothing in Xero.
