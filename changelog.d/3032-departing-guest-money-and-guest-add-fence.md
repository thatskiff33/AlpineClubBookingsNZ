- **A member leaving a booking now always has their own money written down,
  even when somebody else's records are the unreadable ones (#3032).** When a
  removal is held for the office to price, one pending item is raised for each
  guest whose stored nightly prices cannot be read. If the guest who was
  actually leaving had perfectly good records and a guest who was *staying* did
  not, the only item raised was about the guest who stayed — it said "gave back
  no nights", and the honest thing for the office to do with an item like that
  is to close it as nothing to adjust. Doing so cleared the notice, and by then
  the leaving guest's nightly records had been deleted with them, so the amount
  they were owed no longer existed anywhere.

  A held removal now always raises an item for the guest who left, carrying the
  nights they gave back and the prices that were stored against them. Nothing is
  settled automatically and no figure is asserted — what goes back also depends
  on the cancellation terms — but the numbers the office needs are kept instead
  of being thrown away. The item explains that this guest's own records are
  complete and that it is another guest on the booking whose records could not be
  read, so nobody prices it as though the history were missing.

- **Adding a guest to a booking whose money is still being worked out is now
  turned away, instead of quietly changing what the booking costs (#3032).**
  Adding a guest re-prices everybody on the booking and saves the new total. On a
  booking with an amount still under review, that re-price valued the
  unreadable part of the booking at today's rates and folded the difference into
  the saved total — so when the office later confirmed the amount and credited
  the member, the same money had already come off once. The member was credited
  twice for one change.

  The three other ways to change a booking already refused in this situation;
  adding a guest now does the same, with the same plain-English message: the club
  is still working out the money for the last change, the booking is unchanged,
  and please try again once that is finished. Adding a guest to a booking with
  nothing under review is unaffected.
