- **Credit a treasurer uses in Xero is now taken off the member's credit by itself, and put back if the use is undone (#4270).** Until now the app only found out that a member's credit note had been used in Xero (an offset against an invoice that is not a booking's, or a payment made against it) when a booking tried to use that credit and Xero refused. The credit stayed on offer to the member in the meantime. Now, within about 15 minutes of the change in Xero, once the note has been left alone for two minutes, the app writes down the part of the credit that no booking has claimed, so it is no longer offered, and shows an "Admin Adjustment" line on the member's credit history. If the use is undone in Xero, the app gives that credit back. It never takes a member's balance below zero, credit already given to a booking is left to the existing fix for that, nothing is sent to Xero and the member is not emailed. A note that is voided or deleted in Xero is not changed and is reported for an officer to look at. It can be switched off without a deploy (see the upgrade notes).
