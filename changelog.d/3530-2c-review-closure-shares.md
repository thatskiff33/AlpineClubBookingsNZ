- **A booking change whose price a person had to settle now reads honestly on
  its Xero document (#3530, stage 3 of 3).** When a change cannot be priced by
  the system, an officer settles it on a review task — a charge or a refund,
  with a note. Until now the supplementary invoice or credit note that
  followed carried one unexplained line at the figure. It now carries one line
  per settled review share, `Adjustment agreed with member: <the officer's
  note>`, at that share's amount, alongside any lines the change itself
  recorded — and where two shares were settled against one change and its
  invoice was re-stated to their total, both appear. An adjustment is named
  for what it is; it is never dressed up as guest nights.

  As before, a document is itemised only when the recorded lines and shares add
  up exactly to what it bills; otherwise it reads as it always did, with the
  reason recorded on the operation. No amount, Xero key or retry behaviour
  changes.
