- **A lost card dispute that Stripe later reports won now puts the member's refund back on the queue (#5197).**
  While a dispute reads lost, an officer can dismiss or reduce the refund task it covers, because the cardholder already has the money back. If Stripe later turns that loss into a win (a rare "late win"), the money is the club's again and the member is owed it: the app now reopens each refund task dismissed for the loss, with the refund owed again in the club's records, and the "Lost card payment dispute now won" email names each one. It also names any task the app could not reopen, with the reason, so an officer can reopen it under Finance → Payments, and any reduction made for the loss, which is owed to the member again and is left for the treasurer to pay by hand. Reopening such a task by hand now owes the refund again too, and a booking change's refund dismissed for a lost dispute after its booking was cancelled can now be reopened. No money moves by itself.
