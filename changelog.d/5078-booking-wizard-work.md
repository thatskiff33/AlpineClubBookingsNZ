- **The booking form now keeps your work when you visit your profile or refresh (#4950).**
  Your lodge, dates, guests and notes stay in the same browser tab, so following
  **Complete details** or **Family member missing?** to your profile and coming
  back no longer returns you to the first step with an empty party. Dates,
  family details and prices are checked again on return. Dates that have passed
  are dropped and your guests are kept.

  The saved work lives only in that tab, never on the club's servers. Submitting,
  joining the waitlist, saving a draft, sending an exception request, signing out
  or choosing **Start again** clears it. The payment method is not kept, so you
  choose it again at the review step.
