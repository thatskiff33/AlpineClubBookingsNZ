- **Members can record dietary and allergy information on their profile, kept
  private by design (#2941).** A new optional field, **Dietary/allergy
  information**, lets a member of any age note dietary needs or allergies once
  (up to 500 characters). It is **off until the club turns it on** in **Admin →
  Member Fields**; an upgrade leaves it off.

  While it is on, the member sees it on their profile and during onboarding,
  and admins with membership access see and edit it on the member record, when
  creating a member, and in the member CSV import and export. Nobody else sees
  it — not family members, hut leaders, lodge screens, or the booking and
  finance exports — and it is never sent to Xero, the analytics tag,
  notifications or logs. Audit history records only that it changed. Turning
  the field off hides it without deleting anything members recorded, and a
  member's own "Download My Data" export always includes their value.

  The member CSV export contains this health information while the field is
  on, so store and share that file as your privacy policy requires. The
  **Member Fields** page now opens read-only: click **Edit**, change the
  toggles, then **Save** or **Cancel**. A later release shows the value to hut
  leaders for the members on their own stay.
