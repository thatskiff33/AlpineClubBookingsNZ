- **Approving a locked-period change request on a finished stay now applies it
  (#3750).** Until now a Booking Officer could only acknowledge such a request,
  and the panel told them to make the change on the booking page — which cannot
  edit a stay that has already finished, so a guest who stayed but was left off
  the booking could not be added at all.

  When the stay is fully past or completed, **Approve and apply** now applies
  every part of the request: guests added, removed or swapped, and date or
  stay-range changes. Added guests are priced at the stay's own season rates,
  guests already on the booking keep exactly what they paid, and any extra is
  asked for through the ordinary additional payment (card, or internet banking
  against a supplementary Xero invoice) with the usual booking-change email.
  Adding guests carries no change fee; a removal or swap is charged as a
  same-day change under the cancellation policy, and the member can still ask
  for that fee back through a refund request.

  If a night was already full, the officer is asked to confirm the overbooking;
  a whole-lodge hold still refuses. If the booking has changed since the member
  asked, or the season for those nights has been switched off, nothing is
  applied and the request stays pending with the reason. Members still cannot
  edit a finished stay themselves, and a request on a stay that is still running
  is acknowledged exactly as before.
