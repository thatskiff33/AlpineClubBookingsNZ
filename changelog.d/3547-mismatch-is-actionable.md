- **A booking whose recorded guest prices do not add up now asks an officer to
  look, instead of saying it cannot be checked (#3547).** Follow-up to the
  booking money check added in #3278.

  That check tells an officer one of two things: the recorded numbers disagree
  and somebody should look, or the records needed were never kept and there is
  nothing anybody can do. One case was on the wrong side of that line. If a
  guest's nightly prices are all recorded but do not add up to the total
  recorded against that same guest, both numbers are on file and they disagree —
  which is precisely the kind of thing the check exists to surface. It was being
  shown with the quiet "cannot be checked" wording, so nobody looked.

  It is now its own finding, worded as one. The reason that remains on the quiet
  side covers what it actually describes: a guest with no nightly prices
  recorded at all.

  Officers may see a few more bookings asking for review as a result, and those
  are ones where there is genuinely something to resolve.
