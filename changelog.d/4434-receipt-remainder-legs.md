- **A card payment that is larger than one Xero invoice is recorded across the invoices that bill it (#4434).**
  When a later request carries an earlier change, the earlier invoice can move onto the later payment while that payment's receipt is still waiting. The receipt used to record only what the first invoice had left due, and an officer had to enter the rest by hand. It now records the rest on the next invoice that bills the same payment, up to what each invoice has due, and still tells an officer only about money no invoice can take. A credit release already on an invoice is not treated as this receipt's own payment. Nothing is recorded twice, and nothing is posted as a Xero overpayment.
