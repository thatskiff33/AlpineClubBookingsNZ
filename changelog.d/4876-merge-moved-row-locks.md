- **Merging two members no longer fails with an error when someone is signing off an induction or approving a bed for one of them at the same time (#4876).**
  The merge either completes, or tells you nothing was saved and to try again. It no longer shows a server error from the two writes waiting on each other. A booking linked to one of them while the merge has stepped back to wait is included when the merge continues. A booking linked while the merge is already updating one is left unsaved, and you are asked to try again.
