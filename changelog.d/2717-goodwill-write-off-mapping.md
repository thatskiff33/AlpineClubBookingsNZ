- **Goodwill now has its own Xero account, so the accounts can show what the
  club billed and what it chose not to collect (#2717).** When an admin grants
  a member account credit and the member spends it on a booking, that is a cost
  the club chose to bear, not a reduction of what it billed. Until now it was
  booked against the hut-fee-refund account, which netted the two together and
  made the club's income look smaller than it was.

  The Xero setup screen gains a **Goodwill & Write-Offs** mapping. It offers
  expense accounts only, so there is no way to pick the wrong kind by accident,
  and once it is set new goodwill entries post there while revenue stays at the
  billed figure. At year end a committee can say it billed one amount and chose
  not to collect another.

  Credit a member had already paid for is not goodwill and is untouched. A
  cancellation refund, a downward reprice, and credit restored to a member when
  their booking was cancelled all still post to the hut-fee-refund account,
  whichever order things happen in.

- **Clubs that do nothing are unaffected (#2717).** While the new mapping is
  unset, goodwill keeps posting exactly where it posted before. The setup screen
  says so on the row itself, names the account the entries are going to, and
  explains what choosing one would change. Nothing already in Xero is
  reclassified when you do set it — the split applies from that point forward.
