- Goodwill now has its own Xero account. Admin-granted credit applied to an Internet Banking booking is a cost the club chose to bear, not a reduction of what it billed, so the Xero setup screen gains a **Goodwill & Write-Offs** mapping that offers expense accounts only. Revenue then stays at the billed figure and the goodwill shows as its own cost line, which lets a committee say at year end that it billed one amount and chose not to collect another.
- Clubs that do nothing are unaffected. While the new mapping is unset those entries keep posting exactly where they posted before, to the hut-fee-refund account, and the setup screen says so and explains what setting it would change. Ordinary refunds are untouched, and no entry already in Xero is reclassified.
