- **The booking-request pricing panel now pre-fills guest-night rates and shows
  the requester's other-lodge answer (#2749).** On **Admin → Booking Requests →
  Public Requests**, when you price a request **Per guest-night**, each rate
  field (ADULT / YOUTH / CHILD / INFANT) is now **pre-filled from the fees you
  configured** on Admin → Setup & Configuration → Fees, for the season covering
  the stay's check-in. You can still edit any field before saving the quote.

  Just above the note field, the panel now shows **"Member of another Lodge :"**
  with the lodge the requester chose on the public form (or **No**).

  When the requester said they belong to another lodge, the rate fields pre-fill
  at your club's **Full-member** rate for each age group instead of the
  non-member rate — a reciprocal-membership courtesy — and the non-member rate
  they would otherwise have paid is shown in small text under each field for
  reference. The rate used is the check-in night's; a stay spanning two seasons
  with different rates pre-fills the check-in season's rate (the same single
  per-tier value the quote already uses), so adjust it if a stay crosses a
  season boundary.
