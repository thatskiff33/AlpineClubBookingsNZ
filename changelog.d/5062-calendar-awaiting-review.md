- **Bookings awaiting review now appear on the admin booking calendar
  (#4923).** They hold beds, so the calendar's free-bed count already dropped
  for them, but no bar, pill or legend entry showed who held the beds. Awaiting
  Review now has its own bar colour, status pill (on by default), legend entry
  and bookings-list filter option. Admins' saved calendar choices carry over:
  anything they had switched off stays off. A calendar status filter with no
  recognised value now returns no bookings instead of every booking.
