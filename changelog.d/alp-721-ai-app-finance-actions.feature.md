- **A Finance officer can work the finance screens from a connected AI app
  (ALP-721).** Once the club lets officers connect an AI app, an officer who
  holds the Finance admin area can list payments, refund requests, money owed
  back by hand, subscriptions and the finance report; request and approve
  account credit adjustments (a different officer still approves); record a
  booking or subscription paid by hand; run subscription billing; and check
  and repair the Xero sync, links and contacts. A Finance viewer is offered
  the reads only, and an officer without the area is offered none. Refunds and
  anything else that asks for a fresh sign-in, card entry, and the club's
  setup screens (fees, tax, bank details, accounting mappings and connecting
  outside services) stay on the website. Amounts are always cents, and each
  answer leaves out email addresses and Stripe and Xero internal ids. The
  list is in the AI app connections guide. If you named what an AI app
  receives in your privacy notice, the recipient register's `ai-apps` row
  now lists the finance information an officer's connection sends.
