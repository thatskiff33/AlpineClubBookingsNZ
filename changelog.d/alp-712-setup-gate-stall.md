- **Public pages no longer pause for about a second every 15 seconds (#5264).**
  The check for whether the club's site setup is finished used to load the whole website theme and rebuild its stylesheets every 15 seconds, and the visitor who arrived just then waited for it. It now reads only whether setup is finished, does so in the background, and the theme's stylesheets are built once and reused until the club saves a new site style. A brief database outage no longer closes a live club's website; it can still show the "Site setup in progress" screen while the site is first starting up.
