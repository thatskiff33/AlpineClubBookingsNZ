- **The dashboard and the booking page now decide "is this booking's price
  still owed?" in one place.** Nothing a member or administrator sees changes.
  The member dashboard's Payment Owed card and the booking page's payment,
  credit, change-fee and Internet Banking cards each used to repeat that check
  by hand; they now share one, so a future change to what counts as paid
  cannot reach one screen and miss the other.
