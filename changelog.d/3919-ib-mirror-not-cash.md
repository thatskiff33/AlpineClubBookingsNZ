- **Net Collected no longer counts an unpaid bank-transfer booking as money received** (#3372, the owner's review on #3811). When a booking paid by bank transfer was edited down before it was paid, Xero's credit note could mark its payment as partly refunded, and every Net Collected figure (the dashboard card, the Payments tile, Reports and the Finance dashboard) then counted most of the booking's price as collected. A partly or fully refunded payment now counts only when there is a record of the money actually arriving, or it was a card payment, so these unpaid bookings add nothing. Card payments, and bank transfers that were paid and later partly refunded, count exactly as before.
