- **A link from an old club email to a removed booking now says what happened, to the people entitled to know (#5266).** Opening a booking the club has removed used to show the plain "no such page", exactly as if the link had never worked. Now the booking's owner, or a member who was a guest on it, sees "This booking has been cancelled or removed. Contact the club if you need to know more." The same goes for the guest-consent link: the guest it was sent about, or the family adult answering for them, is told the booking was removed.

  Everybody else still sees the plain "no such page", including a Booking Officer or read-only admin who is not on the booking and anyone holding a forwarded or guessed link, so a link tells a stranger nothing. A Full Admin still sees the removed booking's full record. Nothing changes for someone who is signed out: they are asked to sign in first, as before.
