- **An old cancellation refund owed by hand now shows as a refund owed, not as
  a late card charge, after its booking is deleted (#4848).** Refund items
  raised before 19 August 2026 carry no type. One whose booking was later
  deleted was counted in Net Collected and "Refunds owed" as a late card
  charge awaiting the treasurer, though it was a cancellation refund owed by
  hand. The amounts were right and only the line was wrong. Old items are now
  told apart by what they say they are about, the same rule the refund check
  uses since #4843.
