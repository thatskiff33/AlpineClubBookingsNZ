- **A refused booking now brings its reason into view (#4945).** When the club
  refuses a booking from the review step (Continue to Payment, Confirm, Submit
  for Review, Save as Draft or Join Waitlist), or Continue is stopped on the
  guests step, the explanation and any button that fixes it now take focus and
  scroll into view. Before, the message appeared at the top of the page while
  the button you pressed simply reset, so it looked as though nothing had
  happened.

  A "fully booked" refusal brings its waitlist offer into view and announces it
  to screen readers. When a Booking Officer could allow the booking, the message
  links down to the request form. Nothing about what the club refuses, or the
  wording of the refusal, changed.
