- **A bank or cash hand-back is now recorded in the member-money journal when
  the task is raised, and paid there when the treasurer marks it paid
  (#4516).** Such a task cannot be dismissed for now: officers mark it paid
  once the money has gone back, or leave it open. Dismissing with a reason
  comes in a later update. A late card payment the treasurer keeps is recorded as
  already billed where an invoice covers it, and as extra booking revenue only
  for the rest.
