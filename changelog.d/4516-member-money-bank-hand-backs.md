- **A bank or cash hand-back is now recorded in the member-money journal when
  the task is raised, and paid there when the treasurer marks it paid
  (#4516).** Such a task cannot be dismissed for now: officers mark it paid
  once the money has gone back, or leave it open. Dismissing with a reason
  comes in a later update. A card refund shortfall's part to check can still
  be dismissed once the officer has confirmed the card refund reached the
  member, and the refund a cancellation or a booking change handed to the
  treasurer because the cardholder disputed the card payment can be dismissed
  once the dispute is lost for at least that refund; lost for less, officers
  can reduce it by the lost amount and the rest stays owed. A lost dispute is
  counted once across all the refunds on that card payment, oldest first, and
  a refund it is set against cannot be marked paid; each reduction is recorded in the audit
  log as a payment entry, read by those who already read payment entries. Officers are emailed a dispute-specific
  alert for that refund. A late card payment the treasurer keeps is recorded as
  already billed where an invoice covers it, and as extra booking revenue only
  for the rest.
