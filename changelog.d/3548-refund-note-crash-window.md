- **A refund credit note interrupted before its payment is finished on the retry (#3548).** If the app stopped after raising a refund credit note in Xero but before recording the payment that settles it, the retry used to mark the job done with no payment, leaving the card refund's Stripe-account payment missing and nothing on the failures board. The retry now reads the note back from Xero and records the settling payment, or notes that none is due, exactly as the first attempt would. If that payment fails it stays on the failures board for repair, and a note Xero already shows paid or allocated, including one settled by hand, is never paid twice.
