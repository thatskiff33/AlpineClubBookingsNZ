- **A refund credit note interrupted before its payment is finished on the retry, never duplicated (#3548).** If the app stopped after raising a refund credit note in Xero but before recording the payment that settles it, a later retry could mark the job done with no payment, or, days later, raise a second credit note for the same refund. The note is now recorded the moment Xero returns it, and the retry reads it back from Xero: it records the settling payment, dated on the note's own day, or notes that none is due. If that payment fails, the job stays on the failures board for repair. A note Xero already shows paid, part-paid or allocated, including one settled by hand, is never paid twice, and two retries at once record one payment. Older jobs that were closed with no payment are listed in the Xero reconciliation report and by the booking repair tool, which offers a settle that an operator applies by hand after reviewing the note. A part-settled note is listed with the amount still outstanding.
