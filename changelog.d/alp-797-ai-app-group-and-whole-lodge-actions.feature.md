- **A connected AI app can now help with group bookings and whole-lodge
  requests (ALP-797).** With **AI app connections (MCP)** switched on, a
  member's AI app can list the member's own whole-lodge requests, ask the
  booking officer for the whole lodge, withdraw an open request, open a group
  on one of the member's own bookings, read a group's join page by its code,
  and close or reopen the member's own group to new joins. The group actions
  answer **Not Found** while group bookings are switched off. Every change
  needs a connection the member approved for **View and change** and is
  recorded in the audit log. Asking for or withdrawing a whole-lodge request
  can't be undone from the app and the request emails the booking officer, so
  the app is asked to confirm with the member first.

  Joining a group and paying a group's combined bill move money, so they stay
  on the website, as do the visitor and school booking request forms and a
  school's attendee confirmation. A whole-lodge request still tells the member
  nothing about whether the lodge is free. With this, every member page's
  action is either available to an AI app or stays on the website for a
  stated reason. If you named what an AI app receives in your privacy notice,
  the recipient register's `ai-apps` row now lists the new information,
  including a group organiser's first name on a group's join page.
