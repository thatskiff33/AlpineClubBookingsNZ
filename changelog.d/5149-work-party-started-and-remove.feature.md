- **Work parties: started stays, and Remove from work party** (fork #127,
  fork #130). An officer can now add a booking whose stay has already started to a
  work party, for a member at the lodge during the working bee who forgot to
  tick the box. Every night inside the work party's window is discounted,
  including nights already slept; the money comes back as it does for a future
  stay (club credit, or a refund to the card on a card-paid booking). A new
  **Remove from work party** action, shown instead of Add on a booking that is
  on a work party, takes the discount off again: the price goes back up and the
  difference is owed on the booking, like any change that raises the price.
  Credit already given and refunds already paid are left alone. Both go
  through the normal booking change, with no change fee.
