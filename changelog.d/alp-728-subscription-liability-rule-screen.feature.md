- **Choose who owes a subscription on the Subscription lockout page, after seeing who it changes (#5190).**
  The page has a new section, **Who owes a subscription**, that shows your club's liability rule in plain English (or **Not chosen yet**) and lets an officer with both Membership edit and Finance edit choose or change it. Each answer explains what it means for a member, with a worked example for the first question. Before the rule can be saved, **Preview changes** lists, for the current season, who would start owing (newly invoiced, newly asked to pay before booking, or both), who would stop owing (with any unpaid invoice number, for officers with Finance access, to void in Xero if it should not be paid), and which saved "Not required" records the save would reset to "Not invoiced". **Save rule** stays unavailable until a preview of exactly the chosen answers is on screen, and the confirmation says how many records were reset (and says so if records changed after the preview). "Age today" is listed but cannot be chosen yet.
  The list of records to reset is now the same selection the save resets, as it stands when you save: it includes inactive and archived members, marked as such, whose records the save also resets but whom billing does not invoice unless they are made active again. Before, the list left them out. Each record also shows the status the members list gives that member today, so a member the list already shows as "Not invoiced", with the "Marked not required" note, reads the same in the preview.
