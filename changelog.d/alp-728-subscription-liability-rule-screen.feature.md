- **Choose who owes a subscription on the Subscription lockout page, after seeing who it changes (#5190).**
  The page has a new section, **Who owes a subscription**, that shows your club's liability rule in plain English (or **Not chosen yet**) and lets an officer with both Membership edit and Finance edit choose or change it. Each of the two questions explains what its answers mean for a member, with a worked example. Before the rule can be saved, **Preview changes** lists, for the current season, who would start owing (newly invoiced, newly asked to pay before booking, or both), who would stop owing (with any unpaid invoice number, for officers with Finance access, to void in Xero if it should not be paid), and which saved "Not required" records the save would reset to "Not invoiced". **Save rule** stays unavailable until a preview of exactly the chosen answers is on screen, and the confirmation says how many records were reset. "Age today" is listed but cannot be chosen yet.
  The list of records to reset is now exactly what the save resets: it includes inactive and archived members, marked as such, whose records the save also resets but whom billing does not invoice unless they are made active again. Before, the list left them out.
