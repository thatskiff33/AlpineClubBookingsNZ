- **The "Booking Modified" email now tells you when the club is still working
  out the money (#3032).** When a change to your booking saves but the refund or
  charge it earns cannot be established from the club's own records, an officer
  prices it by hand. The email confirming your change used to say nothing at all
  about that: its money section came out empty, which reads as "no money is
  involved" on precisely the change where that is least true.

  The email now says the club is working the amount out and that nothing has
  been refunded or charged yet. It names no figure, because there is not one to
  name — not a zero, not an estimate, and not your booking's new total.

  If the same edit also earns a payment you can make now — one edit can add
  nights that price normally while giving up nights the records cannot value —
  the email carries **both**: the sentence about what is being worked out, and
  the instruction to pay, with its amount, its Xero invoice number and its
  payment reference. Losing that half would leave you told there is nothing to
  do while a hold quietly expired.

  The wording itself shipped earlier in this release; what changes here is that
  every path that emails you about a change now supplies the answer, so the
  email agrees with what your booking page and My Bookings already showed you.
