- **Officers who hold the Content admin area can manage the public website
  through a connected AI app (ALP-723).** Once the club has turned on officer
  connections and the officer has approved the app for officer actions, it can
  read and edit pages, footer sections and banners, the club identity, the
  Contact page role, which fee and policy sections the public pages show, the
  privacy page's hosting details, the site style's colours, fonts and custom
  CSS, and the mountain conditions widget, and list, rename or delete public
  images and photo folders. A view grant reaches the reads only. HTML an app
  sends is cleaned by the same sanitiser as the website's editor. Every change
  but adding an empty photo folder is marked destructive, so the app asks the
  officer first and the call is logged as important. The officer line on the
  approval screen now names the public website's pages and settings too; an
  officer who has already approved an app does not need to approve it again.

  Uploading a file (gallery photos, a library image or the site logo),
  finishing the site setup wizard, and choosing whether the public committee
  roster shows members' photos stay on the website. The AI app connections
  guide (`docs/guides/mcp-server.md`) lists the new actions, and the `ai-apps`
  row of the recipient register says what the Content actions send.
