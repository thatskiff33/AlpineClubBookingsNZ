- **A connected AI app can now manage the member's own account and read the
  club's published information (ALP-718).** With **AI app connections (MCP)**
  switched on, a member's AI app can read and change which club emails they
  receive, update their name, phone number, date of birth, addresses and
  occupation, read a club notice in full and mark it read, list their own
  account history, see who is staying at the lodges they can book (only while
  the lodge roster is switched on, with the same names and nights the roster
  page shows), and read the club's age groups, standard booking messages,
  committee and ski-field report. Changes still need a connection the member
  approved for **View and change**, and each one is recorded in the audit log.

  An app is now sent only the fields each action names, never the rest of what
  the website's screens use, and a connection never acts with an officer's
  powers. Acknowledging a notice, confirming details at onboarding, asking for
  account deletion, turning marketing email on, dietary needs, the lobby-screen
  phone opt-in, the help assistant and the visitor forms stay on the website.
  If you named what an AI app receives in your privacy notice, the recipient
  register's `ai-apps` row now lists the new information.
