- **A failed Xero action now moves you to its explanation, every time (#4975).** On **Admin → Finance → Xero** and **Xero member grouping**, any button you press that fails now shows its message where you can see it and moves the screen and keyboard focus to it: a retry, replay, resolve, sync, contact creation, link repair, family-group creation, mode or rule change, dry run, duplicate scan, disconnect, **Refresh**, or **Refresh from Xero**. Pressing the same button again and getting the same failure brings you back to the message. The message sits just above its card, so it shows even while the card is collapsed, and collapsing and re-opening the card does not pull you back to an old failure. A successful **Refresh** clears the last failure. A list or status that the screen loads by itself, on opening or when you change a filter or page, still shows a quiet notice and leaves focus where it was.
