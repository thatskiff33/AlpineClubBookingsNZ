- **Card disputes (chargebacks) are now recorded, alerted and guarded.** The Stripe webhook handles `charge.dispute.created`, `charge.dispute.funds_withdrawn`, `charge.dispute.funds_reinstated` and `charge.dispute.closed`, keeping one record per dispute. Finance editors get one email when a dispute opens and one when it closes. While a dispute is open or lost, the app issues no card refund for that payment, and cancelling the booking issues no account credit either: it records the cancel, raises a treasurer task naming the dispute, and tells the member the refund is with the treasurer. The booking page and **Finance → Payments** show "Disputed by cardholder" with its outcome. Nothing is posted to Xero for a dispute; `DEPLOYMENT.md` describes recording a lost dispute by hand. Existing deployments must add the four events to their Stripe webhook endpoint once the new release alone serves (see the upgrade note in `DEPLOYMENT.md`).
