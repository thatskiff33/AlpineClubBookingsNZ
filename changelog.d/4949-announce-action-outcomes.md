- **Screen readers now announce what happened after a member or guest acts.** A declined or failed card, a wrong sign-in code, a refused password reset, and a failed request on the profile, booking, contact, booking-request reply, membership-cancellation, nomination and partner-invite screens now appear in an alert that is read aloud and takes focus, instead of a coloured box that some screen readers skipped. Confirmations such as a sent payment link, a recorded response or a sent message are read aloud too. The cancel booking flow keeps focus on each step's heading instead of dropping it to the top of the page, and the reset-password fields say which one failed and why. No wording or amounts changed.
