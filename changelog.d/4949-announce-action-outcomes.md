- **Screen readers now announce what happened after a member or guest acts (#4949).** A declined or failed card, a wrong sign-in code, a refused password reset, and a failed request on the profile, booking, contact, booking-request reply, membership-cancellation, nomination and partner-invite screens now appear in an alert that is read aloud and takes focus, instead of a coloured box that some screen readers skipped. Confirmations such as a sent payment link, a recorded response or a sent message are read aloud too. The cancel booking flow keeps focus on each step's heading instead of dropping it to the top of the page, a refusal that needs a hosting override moves focus to the override reason, and the reset-password fields say which one failed and why. Amounts and payment wording are unchanged; the only new text is screen-reader-only status lines.
