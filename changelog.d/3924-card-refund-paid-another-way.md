- **A card refund Stripe gave up on can now be closed as "Paid another way"** (#3372, the owner's decision of 7 Oct 2026). When the club refunds a card and every retry fails, the refund now stays in Refunds owed and keeps coming off Net Collected until it is settled. If the treasurer pays the member back another way, for example by bank transfer, they can close it under **Card refunds Stripe gave up on** on the Stuck States page. The amount defaults to what is still owed, and a note saying how the member was paid is required. Closing it records the amount as refunded on the payment, takes the refund out of Refunds owed, and writes an audit entry. Stripe is not asked again. For a cancellation's refund, the Xero refund credit note is queued as it would be for a cancellation refunded by bank transfer. Seeing the list needs Finance view access, and closing a refund needs Finance edit.
- **Refunds owed and Net Collected now agree to the cent for every payment** (#3372, from the #3924 money review). A card refund that has sent part of the money now owes only the part still to send. A later, unrelated refund on the same card charge no longer makes an older stuck refund look smaller. If an approved refund request's card refund fails partway, only the unsent part is retried. Refunds owed now reads, payment by payment, exactly what Net Collected took off. Before, a payment owing both a refund by hand and a card refund could show more in Refunds owed than the payment ever held.
- **"Paid another way" now leaves a complete record, and Xero gets the right note** (#3372, the owner's decision of 8 Oct 2026 to ship it with Refunds owed, and the #3924 round-4 review). Closing a card refund now also records it as a refund paid back by hand, so the booking's ledger matches its payment. For a cancellation's card refund, the Xero refund credit note is queued for exactly the amount paid back, worded as a bank transfer. Previously it could come out at nil. No other close raises a note, and the nightly Xero check no longer raises a card note settled from the Stripe account for money paid back by bank. The dialog now says, before you close, whether a Xero note will be raised. It also says that a refund made in the Stripe dashboard should not be closed here, and that paying back less than is owed ends the refund. A refund whose last retry failed with a timeout or network error is flagged "Stripe may have refunded: check the Stripe dashboard first". A refund recorded on the payment while the close is running now stops the close, rather than letting the member be paid twice.
- **Refunds owed now includes old group cancellation card refunds as one club-wide amount** (#3372, the owner's decision of 8 Oct 2026). Before #3653, a group cancellation was refunded as one card refund that belongs to no single booking. If that refund is still unpaid, what it still has to send now counts in Refunds owed as one amount for the whole club. It is not counted against any booking or in Net Collected. A refund owed by hand on a payment that never showed money taken also counts in Refunds owed now.
- **A card refund that fails partway is retried only for what it has not sent** (#3372, #3924 round-4 review). If Stripe refunded a slice but the app could not record it, the retry now replays that slice and records Stripe's original refund. Before, it could ask again for slices already recorded. A card refund closed earlier no longer takes a later refund as its own, so a still-open refund is no longer shown as owing more than it does.
