- **A card refund Stripe gave up on can now be closed as "Paid another way" (#3372).** When the club refunds a card and every retry fails, the refund stays in Refunds owed and keeps coming off Net Collected until it is settled. If the treasurer pays the member back another way, for example by bank transfer, they close it under **Card refunds Stripe gave up on** on the Stuck States page. Seeing the list needs Finance view access, and closing a refund needs Finance edit.

  The dialog asks the treasurer to choose **Paid back in full** or **Paid back part of it - the rest will no longer be owed**; it never guesses from the amount. In full, the amount is what is still owed. In part, the treasurer types an amount below that, and the dialog says in dollars how much will stop being owed. A part payment is final: the rest is no longer owed anywhere, and a later refund on the booking pays only its own share. A note saying how the member was paid back is always required. A refund that replaces a superseded payment can only be closed in full.

  Closing it records the amount as refunded on the payment and in the booking's ledger, takes the refund out of Refunds owed, and writes an audit entry saying whether it was paid in full or in part. Stripe is not asked again. Every kind of card refund now gets a Xero refund credit note for exactly the amount paid back, worded as a bank transfer, as long as there is a Xero invoice to credit. A late card charge - one taken after its booking was cancelled, which the treasurer chose to refund - is credited against its own record in Xero, never the booking's invoice. If Xero has no record of the charge yet, closing the refund first records it, as an invoice for the whole charge paid into the Stripe account on the day Stripe took it, and the bank-transfer refund credit note follows once that record is in Xero. Xero then shows the money into the Stripe account and the money out of the bank, and the charge nets to nil. The dialog says before you close what will be recorded in Xero, and the message afterwards says what was queued. The nightly Xero check no longer raises a card note, settled from the Stripe account, for money that went back by bank.

  A refund whose last retry failed with a timeout, a network or Stripe server error, or Stripe saying the money was already refunded is flagged "Stripe may have refunded: check the Stripe dashboard first". If Stripe did refund it after all and the refund reaches the app after the close, the payment now shows under a new **Card refunds paid back twice** list on the Stuck States page, so the treasurer can recover the extra money. If the list changes while the dialog is open, the dialog follows it: it closes if the refund is no longer waiting, and resets the amount and says so if what is owed changed.
- **Refunds owed and Net Collected now agree to the cent for every payment (#3372).** A card refund that has sent part of the money now owes only the part still to send. A later, unrelated refund on the same card charge no longer makes an older stuck refund look smaller. If an approved refund request's card refund fails partway, only the unsent part is retried. Refunds owed now reads, payment by payment, exactly what Net Collected took off. Before, a payment owing both a refund by hand and a card refund could show more in Refunds owed than the payment ever held.
- **Refunds owed now includes old group cancellation card refunds as one club-wide amount (#3372).** Before #3653, a group cancellation was refunded as one card refund that belongs to no single booking. If that refund is still unpaid, what it still has to send now counts in Refunds owed as one amount for the whole club. It is not counted against any booking or in Net Collected. A refund owed by hand on a payment that never showed money taken also counts in Refunds owed now.
- **A card refund that fails partway is retried only for what it has not sent (#3372).** If Stripe refunded a slice but the app could not record it, the retry now replays that slice and records Stripe's original refund. Before, it could ask again for slices already recorded. A closed card refund no longer takes a later refund as its own, so a still-open refund is no longer shown as owing more than it does.
