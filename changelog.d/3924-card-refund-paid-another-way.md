- **A card refund Stripe gave up on can now be closed as "Paid another way"** (#3372, the owner's decision of 7 Oct 2026). When the club refunds a card and every retry fails, the refund now stays in Refunds owed and keeps coming off Net Collected until it is settled. If the treasurer pays the member back another way, for example by bank transfer, they can close it under **Card refunds Stripe gave up on** on the Stuck States page. The amount defaults to what is still owed, and a note saying how the member was paid is required. Closing it records the amount as refunded on the payment, takes the refund out of Refunds owed, and writes an audit entry. Stripe is not asked again. For a cancellation's refund, the Xero refund credit note is queued as it would be for a cancellation refunded by bank transfer. Seeing the list needs Finance view access, and closing a refund needs Finance edit.
- **Refunds owed and Net Collected now agree to the cent for every payment** (#3372, from the #3924 money review). A card refund that has sent part of the money now owes only the part still to send. A later, unrelated refund on the same card charge no longer makes an older stuck refund look smaller. If an approved refund request's card refund fails partway, only the unsent part is retried. Refunds owed now reads, payment by payment, exactly what Net Collected took off. Before, a payment owing both a refund by hand and a card refund could show more in Refunds owed than the payment ever held.
