- **The Xero health check no longer slows down every season (#4936).** Its count of card refunds with no matching Xero credit note used to re-check every card payment the club had ever refunded, one at a time, so **Admin → Finance → Xero**, the stuck-states page and the finance sync-health tab took longer to open each year. It now skips, in one database read, the payments whose credit notes already cover the full refunded amount, and checks only the rest in detail. The count and the list of refunds it shows are unchanged, and so is the nightly self-heal that raises missing notes.
