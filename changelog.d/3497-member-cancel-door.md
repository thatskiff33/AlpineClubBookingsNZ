- **A member on a waitlist can now cancel their booking from the booking page
  (#3497).** Pressing **Cancel** on a waitlisted booking, or on a booking holding
  a waitlist offer, used to dead-end on a message saying only certain kinds of
  booking could be cancelled — because "which bookings can be cancelled?" was
  written down in three places that disagreed. There is now one list, and the
  Cancel button, the refund preview and the cancel action all read it.

  A booking that is **with the club for review** is the one deliberate
  exception: a member is not offered Cancel on it, and if they try they are told
  to contact the club. Cancelling it directly would have left a cancelled
  booking sitting in the officers' Approvals queue; the reviewing officer's
  Reject withdraws it and clears the queue in one step. Officers and admins
  working from the request and review screens are unaffected. The booking notes
  editor also now saves on a paid booking, where before it was offered and then
  refused.
