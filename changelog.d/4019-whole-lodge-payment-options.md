- **An approved whole-lodge booking can now be paid by card, with account
  credit, or by internet banking — the member chooses (#4019).**
  Approving a member's whole-lodge request used to settle the question for
  them: the booking was confirmed with an internet-banking invoice already
  raised, so the booking page offered only the bank-transfer instructions and
  the card option every ordinary booking has was never shown.

  With the **Internet Banking payments** and **Xero** modules both on, approval
  now confirms the booking and holds the lodge without picking a payment
  method. The member is emailed the amount owing and pays from their booking
  page, where they see the same choices as any other booking: pay by card, or
  **Pay by internet banking instead**, which raises the Xero invoice at that
  moment. The request form gains a **Put my account credit towards this
  booking if it is approved** tick for members who hold credit; on approval as
  much of their credit as the total allows is applied, they pay only the
  remainder, and credit that covers the whole price settles the booking as paid
  on the spot. The officer's queue row says when a member asked for this, and
  the approval toast says where the money was left.

  With either module off nothing changes: the approval still raises a
  pay-on-account invoice through Xero, or emails admins to invoice by hand, as
  before. Members can only switch a whole-lodge booking to internet banking
  inside the club's usual internet-banking cut-off before check-in, the same
  rule ordinary bookings follow.

  Starting card entry and leaving it unfinished keeps the booking eligible for
  excess-credit returns after a price reduction and for the unpaid-stay queue.
  Concurrent switches to internet banking create one receivable, and pressing
  the switch again queues the Xero invoice if the first press could not. Officer
  messages now reflect current payment modules and describe zero-price paid
  approvals without attributing them to credit.
