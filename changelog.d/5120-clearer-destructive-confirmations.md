- **Admin delete and bulk-change prompts now say what will happen, and
  revoking a lobby screen asks first (#4973).** The bulk member dialog names
  the selected members (the first ten, then "and N more", including members
  ticked on other pages) and states each action's effect. Deactivate sets each
  leaving date to today, removes each member from their family groups and
  clears future shared-room bed placements. Reactivate clears the leaving date
  but restores neither the family groups nor the placements. Change Access
  replaces each member's existing access roles with the one picked, and says
  that choosing Organisation also sets the age tier to N/A.

  **Revoke** on Display Devices now opens a confirmation warning that the
  screen returns to pairing and must be paired again at the lodge. Deleting a
  season names it and warns that its nightly rates go with it and that it
  cannot be undone, on both the Seasons page and Hut Fees. Deleting a family
  group names the group and says what goes with it (billing-family choices,
  requests, partner invitations and invoiced markers). A failed group delete
  now shows its reason above the group list and takes focus, instead of doing
  nothing.

  Nothing the server does changed. Only what the officer is told before and
  after.
