- **Switching a card booking to internet banking now asks first.** Tapping
  **Pay by internet banking instead** on an unpaid booking opens a confirm
  dialog that says an invoice will be emailed and the member cannot switch back
  to paying by card from the booking page. Choosing **Keep paying by card**
  changes nothing. The dialog's wording is club-editable under Booking Detail
  (**Confirm switch to Internet Banking**, with its own version for an approved
  whole-lodge booking) (#4958).
