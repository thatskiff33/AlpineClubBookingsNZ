- **A connected AI app can now help with lodge life (ALP-796).** With **AI app
  connections (MCP)** switched on, a member's AI app can list club events,
  read the message board and post plain text to it, see the food order form,
  place a food order and list the member's own orders, read the member's own
  lodge induction, list and read work parties (including the ones a stay
  overlaps), and send a maintenance report. Each answers **Not Found** while
  its module is off, as the website does. Posting, ordering and reporting
  still need a connection the member approved for **View and change**, and
  each one is recorded in the audit log. Because each tells other people, the
  app is asked to confirm with the member first. The same now applies to
  confirming a family member's details, which changes another person's
  record.

  A connection acts only as the member: it sees their own food orders and
  inductions even when the person is on the committee, a hut leader or an
  admin, and an organisation account's connection is refused what the
  account itself is refused. Message-board pictures and formatting, sharing a
  post with other clubs, maintenance photos, signing off an induction, the
  chore roster and maintenance QR links, problem reports and the lodge door
  codes stay on the website. If you named what an AI app receives in your
  privacy notice, the recipient register's `ai-apps` row now lists the new
  information, including other members' names on message-board posts.

  **Book a Stay offers work parties to members only.** The "I am attending a
  working bee" choice, like the rest of work parties, is now shown only to
  members: a non-member login or an organisation account sees no work party to
  choose and books as before. It lists work parties up to two years ahead.
