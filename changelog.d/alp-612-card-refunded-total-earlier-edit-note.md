- **A card payment's refunded total keeps an earlier booking edit's credit note when a later note syncs from Xero, and never rises above what Stripe recorded (#4444).**
  The refunded-total recount now finds every payment's edit credit notes through its Xero invoice, not only a bank transfer's. Before, a card payment with two edit notes could read only the latest one and raise a false "Xero may be missing a credit note" alert to the treasurer. A note the payment also links as its own refund note is counted once. By the owner's decision of 23 Nov 2026, a card payment's recounted total is now capped at the refunds Stripe recorded, as an organiser-paid child's already was, so an old card refund recorded as two Xero notes can't count twice. Where Xero shows a refund before Stripe's sync arrives, the total waits for that sync. The Stripe raise-only floor and the bank-transfer floor are unchanged.
