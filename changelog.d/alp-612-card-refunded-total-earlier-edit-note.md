- **A card payment's refunded total keeps an earlier booking edit's credit note when a later note syncs from Xero (#4444).**
  The refunded-total recount now finds every payment's edit credit notes through its Xero invoice, not only a bank transfer's. Before, a card payment with two edit notes could read only the latest one, raising a false "Xero may be missing a credit note" alert to the treasurer, or leaving the total understated until Stripe recorded the refund. A note the payment also links as its own refund note is counted once. The Stripe raise-only floor, the bank-transfer floor and the organiser-child cap are unchanged.
