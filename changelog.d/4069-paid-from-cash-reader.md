- **The "Your Request Was Approved" email now says what is really left to pay (#4069).**
  When an officer approves a booking-rule exception that creates a new booking, the email's payment line is worked out from the money the club has actually received for that booking, not from the booking's status. It names what the booking costs less account credit applied and less money already held, and says whether the beds are held while it waits. A booking already covered (for example wholly by account credit) gets no payment line instead of being asked for money. A payment still pending, a refund made or owed, and a card charge that was replaced and is being refunded no longer count as money held. A stay a group organiser pays for says so and asks the member for nothing. Where the records cannot prove a figure (older back-filled payments, a disputed card payment, an older bank-transfer payment recorded without the amount the bank actually showed), the email names no amount and sends the member to the booking or the club rather than guessing.
  Nothing else about a booking changes: no payment, refund, credit or status is written, and every other screen and email still reads what it read before. A club that has customised this email keeps its wording; an override that prints `{{amount}}` alone should keep `{{paymentNote}}` beside it, because `{{amount}}` is now empty when no figure can be vouched for.
