- **Download the booking's Xero invoice and credit notes (#5066).** When the club's Xero module is on, the booking page offers **Download invoice** beside **Edit Booking** once the club has raised the booking's Xero invoice: it saves Xero's own PDF, so members, admins and booking officers get the one invoice of record. A new **Invoice and credits** card lists each Xero credit note raised against the booking, each with its own download, a short summary from the booking's own records (booking total, account credit applied, paid less refunds, still to pay), and a plain-words line when the invoice in Xero does not show the latest change. Downloaded files start with the club's short name. A booking that also has an invoice for a later change shows a line asking the member to contact the club for copies instead. A guest on someone else's booking, a cancelled booking and a place the group organiser paid for by internet banking get no invoice, and nothing is offered while Xero is off. Each download is recorded in the audit log under Booking.
