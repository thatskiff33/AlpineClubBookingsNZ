- **Download the booking's Xero invoice and credit notes (#5066).** When the club's Xero module is on, the booking page offers **Download invoice** beside **Edit Booking** once the club has raised the booking's Xero invoice: it saves Xero's own PDF, so members, admins and booking officers get the one invoice of record. A new **Invoice and credits** card lists each Xero credit note raised against the booking, each with its own download, a short summary from the booking's own records (booking total, any change fee, account credit applied, paid less refunds and credits returned, anything kept under the booking's change terms, and what is still to pay or due once reviewed), and a plain-words line when the club's accounts do not show the latest change: the invoice could not be updated, a credit for the change has not been made, or the invoice stays as it was issued because it can no longer be changed (for example once it is paid). Downloaded files start with the club's short name. A booking that also has an invoice for a later change shows a line asking the member to contact the club for copies instead. A guest on someone else's booking, a cancelled booking and a place the group organiser paid for by internet banking get no invoice, and nothing is offered while Xero is off. Each download is recorded in the audit log under Booking.
