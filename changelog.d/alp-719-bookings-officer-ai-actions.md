- **Officers with Bookings & Beds can now use a connected AI app for that area's admin work (ALP-719).**
  95 new actions cover booking search and the officer tools on a booking, group, school and whole-lodge requests, change and exception requests, booking reviews, the waitlist, occupancy, promo codes, bed allocation, rooms and beds, seasons, booking policies and the area's settings. Each is offered only to an officer who holds the permission its route needs (Bookings & Beds view or edit, or Finance edit to withdraw an extra payment request), and the route still checks the officer's permissions as for the browser. The nine steps that ask an officer to prove who they are again stay website-only, and so does saving the member-guest settings, which decides whether members' names can be browsed (owner decision D-18). A connection receives only the fields each action names; the AI app guide and the privacy register now say which other members' information an officer's connection sends. Nothing changes until a club switches on **Officers may connect an AI app**.
