- **Parent and partner relationships can no longer contradict each other
  (#3291).** A pending or confirmed partner pair cannot also be recorded as a
  direct parent and dependant. Member requests, admin linking, family-request
  approval, application mapping, and member merge now check both directions
  before saving.

  Conflicting records stay unchanged and the relevant review remains available
  for correction. Privacy-safe partner requests still return their generic
  response, and claiming a family invitation still completes the family join
  when only its optional partner link is refused.
