- **A bulk email to members now asks you to confirm the audience and count
  before it sends (#4964).** On **Admin → Members → Communications**, clicking
  **Send to Members**, or pressing Enter in the Subject, used to email everyone
  in the audience at once, with no chance to check who would get it.

  The page now counts the people the audience reaches who opted in to marketing
  email and asks, for example, *Send this email to 312 people?*, naming the
  audience and the subject. Cancel has the focus, so a stray Enter sends
  nothing, and checking the count does not use up the hourly send limit. If
  nobody would receive it, or the count cannot be read, nothing is offered.

  A line under **Recipients** now says who each audience reaches: *All Active
  Members* includes lodge, school and non-member booking contact accounts,
  *Members Only* leaves out accounts with the Full Admin role (officers on other
  admin roles are included), and *Admins Only* reaches Full Admin accounts only.
  Who each audience reaches has not changed. The result of a send is now
  announced to screen readers.
