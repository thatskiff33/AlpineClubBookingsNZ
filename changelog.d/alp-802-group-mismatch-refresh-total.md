- **The Xero contact group mismatch Refresh no longer says everything was re-synced when only the first 200 were.**
  A Refresh on Admin → Finance → Xero health diagnostics re-syncs at most 200 flagged contacts. With more flagged, its success line read "Re-synced 200 of 200". It now counts out of the true total and says to refresh again, for example "Re-synced 200 of 340 flagged contacts from Xero at 9:15 am. Refresh again for the rest." At or under 200 the line is unchanged.
