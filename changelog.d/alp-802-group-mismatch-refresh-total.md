- **The Xero contact group mismatch Refresh no longer says everything was re-synced when the 200 limit left some out.**
  A Refresh on Admin → Finance → Xero health diagnostics re-syncs at most 200 flagged contacts of each kind (mismatches and members to review by hand). With more flagged, its success line read "Re-synced 200 of 200". It now counts out of the true total and says how to reach the rest, for example "Re-synced 200 of 340 flagged contacts from Xero at 9:15 am. The other 140 were not re-fetched; Refresh Xero Groups on the members list (or Refresh from Xero on Xero member grouping) re-reads every contact's groups." Another Refresh would re-fetch the same first 200, so the line points to the full groups refresh instead. The total counts distinct Xero contacts, so members sharing one contact count once. At or under the limit the line is unchanged.
