- **An old open edit review no longer stops a member or officer changing a booking's money** (#4344, wave C2). Since every edit is priced from stored data (#4317) and the edit-review clean-up left no review open (#4343), the check that refused a date change, a guest added or removed, a batch edit or a promotion re-price with "The club is still working out the money for the last change to this booking" is gone, along with every sign of a review still pending: the booking page's "Your booking change is saved" banner and its editable "Adjustment being reviewed" message (a club's saved wording for it is no longer used), the "· being checked" mark and "Adjustment being checked" chip on My Bookings, the Transaction History note, the payment-link note, the change email's review sentence, the admin "Money waiting for review" warning and the AI app's `priceBeingChecked` field. Completed and dismissed reviews stay on the booking's history, in the audit log and in Xero as before.
