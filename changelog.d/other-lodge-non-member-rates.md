- **Other lodges follow Alpine Central Server API 2.2: non-member rates.** On **Admin → Lodges → Other lodges**, your lodge's editor now has a **Non-member rates** grid: what a non-member pays per person per night (NZD incl. GST) in **winter** and **summer** for each guideline age group — under 5, 5–11, 12–17 and 18+. The rates are uploaded with your lodge's other details and appear on the central server's public lodge search, where non-members are sent to your own booking site to book; nothing is charged from them here. Rates for other clubs' lodges are downloaded and kept as they are sent (#61).

  **This site now requires central server version 2.2.** Syncing only runs while the two versions are identical, so upgrade this site and the central server in the same window; until both are on 2.2 syncing pauses and the Alpine Central Server setup page shows both numbers. The database change only adds columns and needs no maintenance window (`docs/BLUE_GREEN_MIGRATION_SAFETY.tsv`).
