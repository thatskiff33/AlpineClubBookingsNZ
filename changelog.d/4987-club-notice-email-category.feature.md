- **Club notices now reach every member who has not turned them off (#4987).**
  Bulk email is now one of two kinds. A **club notice** — an AGM notice, a lodge
  closure, subscriptions due — goes to every member in the chosen group except
  those who turn off the new **Club notices** preference on their profile. A
  **club newsletter** still goes only to members who turned on **Club
  newsletters** (the old marketing preference, unchanged). Members who never
  saved their email preferences will now receive club notices, where before
  they received no bulk email at all.

  In **Admin → Members → Communications** you choose the kind for each message
  before you can send, and the page shows how many members it will reach first.
  The confirmation and the audit record now tell members who opted out apart
  from members who never opted in. A notice emailed with **Also email this
  notice to the audience** is always a club notice. The database change only
  adds a column that starts on.
