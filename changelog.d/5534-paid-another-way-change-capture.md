- **A late booking-change payment refunded by bank is recorded once in Xero (#4381).**
  When a treasurer closes the refund of a late card payment for a booking change as "Paid another way", and the booking's Xero invoice already bills that change, the app now records the payment against that invoice and credits the bank refund against it. It no longer raises a second invoice that counted the money as income twice and left the original invoice owing. The Xero repair tool lists such a payment when nothing in Xero records it yet, and names one it cannot match to a booking change so an officer can record it by hand. Where another Xero document already records the whole payment (the club's credit-release sweep until #4380 lands, the booking's own invoice, or another invoice covering the change), no bank refund note is raised in Xero: record that note by hand. Closes made before this change are not repaired automatically.
