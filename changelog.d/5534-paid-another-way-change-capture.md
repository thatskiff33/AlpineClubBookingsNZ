- **A late booking-change payment refunded by bank is recorded once in Xero (#4381).**
  When a treasurer closes the refund of a late card payment for a booking change as "Paid another way", and the booking's Xero invoice already bills that change, the app now records the payment against that invoice and credits the bank refund against it. It no longer raises a second invoice that counted the money as income twice and left the original invoice owing. The Xero repair tool lists such a payment when nothing in Xero records it yet, and names one it cannot match to a booking change so an officer can record it by hand. Where one Xero invoice already records the whole payment (the booking's own invoice, or another invoice covering the change), the bank refund note is raised against that invoice when the refund is closed. Where that cannot be told for certain, the dialog says to record the refund by hand, and the repair tool lists every such close with no bank refund note raised or queued. Where the payment is held as released credit, no Xero refund note is raised yet: one follows automatically once that credit is released (#4380), so the dialog and the repair tool say not to record it by hand, which would credit it twice. Closes made before this change are not repaired automatically.
