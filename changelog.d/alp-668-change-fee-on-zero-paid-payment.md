- **A late-notice change fee on a booking paid with account credit or a promotion is now recorded on its payment (#5039).**
  When a member moved the dates of a paid booking that cost them nothing in cash, because credit or a promotion covered it, and the move made the stay cheaper or cost only the fee, the fee showed on the change history, the Xero invoice and the booking ledger but not on the payment. The amount owed was short by the fee. It is now recorded on the payment once, as for a booking paid by card or bank transfer. Bookings changed before this release are not corrected.
  Credit clamping also includes the newly recorded fee when payment has been captured but the booking is still pending. A missing payment row now produces an error log if an edit attempts to record a fee.
