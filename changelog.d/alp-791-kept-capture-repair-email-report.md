- **The booking-vs-Xero repair tool no longer says an officer was emailed when the email did not go out.**
  When the repair tool records a kept late card payment whose retired change invoice could not be re-issued, it marks that invoice captured and emails an officer. Its result line said "an officer emailed" even when the send failed. It now says what happened: an officer was emailed, the email did not send and is queued to retry, nobody receives Xero sync alerts, the club's email delivery settings skipped it, or the email could not be sent. The server log written when a kept payment's revived change invoice gets no receipt now words its message to fit every reason, and warns where the invoice may be left unpaid in Xero.
