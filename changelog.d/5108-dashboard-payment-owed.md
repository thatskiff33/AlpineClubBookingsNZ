- **The dashboard's Payment Owed card now shows what the booking page asks you
  to pay** (#4944). It used to add up each unpaid booking's full price, so a
  member who had applied account credit, or whose booking carried a recorded
  change fee, saw a different figure on the dashboard from the booking's Amount
  due. The total, the "N bookings need payment" count and the link now read the
  same figure as the booking page: the price plus any recorded change fee, less
  the credit already applied. A booking its credit fully covers is no longer
  counted. What is charged is unchanged.
