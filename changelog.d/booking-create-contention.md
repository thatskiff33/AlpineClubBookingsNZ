- **A booking that fails only because the system is busy now says so, and asks the member to try again (#4933).** When many members book at once, or an officer runs a long job such as assigning beds across a busy week, a new booking has to wait its turn. It used to give up after 5 seconds and show "Failed to create booking", which read as a hard refusal. It now waits up to 30 seconds, the same allowance the longest of those jobs has. If it still cannot get in, the member sees "The booking system is busy right now. Nothing was saved, so please try again in a moment." Nothing is saved, charged or emailed in that case. This covers ordinary bookings, saved drafts and waitlist joins. Under a heavy burst a waiting booking holds one of the web server's database connections for longer, which `docs/CONCURRENCY_AND_LOCKING.md` describes; no settings change is needed.
