- **Download My Data now gives a member their whole record, and Full Admins can
  produce the same file for anyone who asks (#4915).** The member's download used
  to be a hand-picked list that missed records added since it was written, cut
  activity off at 500 entries, and promised "all data". It now covers every kind
  of personal record the club keeps, or names what it leaves out and why, in the
  file itself. It shows only the member's own rows: on a booking someone else
  made, it shows their own guest row and never the other guests or the booker.
  The file is named after the club, and the profile wording says what the file
  includes and that the privacy officer can supply anything else.

  Full Admins have a new **Subject access report** card in a member's
  **Lifecycle & Deletion** section. It produces the same file for that member
  (dependants included), for anyone found by email (an applicant or someone who
  sent a booking request), or for a non-member guest picked from a name search,
  since guest records hold no email address. An email shared by more than one
  member record is refused, so you open the right member's page instead. Each
  report asks you to confirm your sign-in, is limited to 20 per officer a day,
  and is recorded in the audit log under Privacy. The person the report is about
  also sees that row in their own activity, so a copy released by mistake does
  not go unnoticed. The member's own downloads are now recorded in their
  activity as well.

  Records filed by an address shared with another member are withheld for
  manual review, including for a member who can sign in. The file includes the
  person's induction signer assignments, sign-offs and guest chore duties.
  Activity follows the same subject and visibility rules as their own timeline.

  The starter privacy policy's access section is updated to match. A club whose
  policy still has the original starter text gets the new wording; a policy the
  club has edited is left alone. The personal-data retention guide has a new
  section, "Answering an access request", on checking who is asking before you
  send a file.
