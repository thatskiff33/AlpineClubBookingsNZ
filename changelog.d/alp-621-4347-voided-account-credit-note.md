- **An account-credit note voided in Xero now stays voided, and its refund stays uncounted, when an older Xero reading arrives afterwards (#4347).**
  When a cancelled booking's money is kept as account credit, the app raises an account-credit note in Xero. If the treasurer voided that note, an older Xero reading of it that still said "live" could reach the app afterwards, mark the note live again and count the money as refunded again. On a $200 Internet Banking payment with $100 kept as account credit, the payment went back to showing $100 refunded after the void had taken it to $0. The app now applies the same rule it already used for refund credit notes: a void always wins, and between two live readings the one Xero updated last wins. The older reading also no longer posts a spend of that credit to the member's other booking, stamps the member's cancellation credit, or mirrors the note's spend. The note's link stays active or inactive as it was. Card payments' refund totals are unchanged. No migration. Notes an older reading already revived before this release are not corrected or reported by it; a booking's Sync from Xero now applies the current void.
