- **A Full Admin can unarchive a member (#5540).**
  On the member's page, under **Lifecycle & Deletion**, an archived member now has an **Unarchive Member** action for Full Admins. It takes a reason and needs no second admin. It clears the archive only: the member goes back to cancelled, still inactive and unable to sign in, keeps their leaving date, and does not get back the family links the archive removed. No email is sent and nothing changes in Xero. A new **Archive history** list on the same card shows every archive (who requested and approved it, when and why) and every unarchive, newest first, across repeated cycles. See [unarchiving a member](../docs/guides/membership-cancellations.md#unarchive-a-member).
