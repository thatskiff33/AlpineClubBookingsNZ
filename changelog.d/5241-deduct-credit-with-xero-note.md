- **An officer can now take back credit that already has a Xero credit note, and the app corrects Xero in the same approval (#5241).**
  Since v0.14.0 such a deduction was refused, so a credit granted by mistake or for the wrong amount could not be fixed in the app once its note existed. Approving the deduction now holds the money off the member's balance at once and queues one Xero correction. If the whole of an unused note is taken back, and the note backs no other credit, the note is voided. Otherwise the app raises an unemailed invoice for only the amount taken back, on the member's contact with the note's account and tax rate, and allocates it to the note, so the note's remaining credit matches the member's credit in the app to the cent; earlier uses of the note are kept. Credit with no note is taken first. A retry after a failure finds what already reached Xero and applies it once. While the correction runs the member's credit card shows **Waiting for Xero correction**, and the member sees the deduction and its reason once Xero agrees. **Cancel** gives the credit back, but only while Xero holds nothing of the correction; after that the officer retries, or voids the correction invoice in Xero and then cancels. A deduction is still refused while the credit's own Xero note is being raised. The opening-credit batch reversal still refuses noted credit. Officer guide: [Deducting credit that has a Xero note](../docs/guides/refund-requests.md#deducting-credit-that-has-a-xero-note).
