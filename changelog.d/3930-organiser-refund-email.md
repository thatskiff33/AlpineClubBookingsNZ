- **A joiner's "Booking Modified" email now says where an organiser-paid refund
  went.** When a group booking's organiser paid for a joiner's place by card and
  the joiner's booking is edited down, the reduction is refunded to the
  organiser's card (#3653). The joiner's email used to say the refund had gone
  "to your original payment method"; it now says it was processed to the group
  organiser's card, because the organiser paid for the booking. Refunds on every
  other booking keep their existing wording (#3916).
