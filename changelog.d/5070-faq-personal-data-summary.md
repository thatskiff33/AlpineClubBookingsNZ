- **The starter FAQ now lists more of the personal data the club holds, and points
  to the full list (#4914 follow-up).** The answer to "What personal data does
  the club hold about me?" now also names addresses, profile photo, dietary or
  allergy information, gender, occupation, title, family relationships and
  membership history, says it is a summary, and points to section 2 of the
  Privacy Policy for the full list.

  New clubs get the new text. A club whose FAQ still has the exact starter text
  is updated automatically on upgrade. **If your club has edited its FAQ,
  review that answer**; an edited FAQ is not changed. **If your club has edited
  its privacy page, check that section 2 lists the categories the answer names**,
  because the new answer sends members there. `docs/UPGRADING.md` has the
  post-upgrade check.
