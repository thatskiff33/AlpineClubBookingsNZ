- **When a waitlist offer lapses, the place goes to the next person (#5410).**
  An offer that ran out used to be offered straight back to the same member in
  the same run, because they were still first in the queue. A member who was
  not reading their email could hold a freed place for offer after offer while
  everyone behind them waited.

  Now the place is offered to the next party behind them that fits. The member
  whose offer lapsed goes back to their original place and keeps their number,
  and is considered last for that place, so a member alone on the waitlist is
  still offered it again. Anyone else whose offer lapsed in the same run is
  considered just before them, at whichever lodge, after everyone who has not
  just had a turn, so
  two members who let offers lapse together do not swap the places while the
  people behind them wait. If offers keep lapsing, the place moves on down the
  queue and only returns to the front once everyone has had a turn.

  A lapsed cross-lodge offer passes on at the lodge that was offered. At a club
  that serves each lodge's own queue first, a member whose offer just lapsed
  now goes behind members from other lodges who opted into that lodge and have
  not had the offer; every other offer still serves the lodge's own queue
  first.

  Nothing is stored about the lapse. When a different place frees on the same
  nights later (a cancellation, say), the member who let an offer lapse is
  considered in their usual place and can be offered it.

  The "Waitlist Offer Expired" email now says a place that is not taken up is
  offered to the next person, that this one may go to someone behind them, and
  that they keep their place in the queue; its "New Position" line now reads
  "Your Position", and so does the same line in the "Your Waitlist Place Is
  Back" email, which also shows the member's existing place in the queue. A
  club that has saved its own wording for either email keeps that wording; use
  **Restore Default** on the email's template page to take the new text.
