- **Two Other lodges may now have the same name; the Alpine Central Server's id tells them apart ([triyder/AlpineClubBookingsNZ#69](https://github.com/triyder/AlpineClubBookingsNZ/issues/69)).** The central server (now **API 2.5**) accepts two lodges of one name, for example two clubs' "Ruapehu Lodge". This site decides which lodge is yours by its central id rather than its name, so renaming your lodge keeps it yours and another club's lodge of the same name is never merged into it or edited as yours. Your lodge can be renamed to a name another lodge already has. Where a lodge is chosen from a list (the booking-request form, the visiting-club rate picker and **Central lodge** on a lodge's setup), two lodges of the same name (or names that only look alike) show their location as well, for example "Ruapehu Lodge (Whakapapa)"; so do the **Edit** buttons on the Other lodges list when two of your lodges share a name, and the "Member of another lodge" line in the booking-request queue always shows the lodge's location. A lodge here that has not yet been linked to the central server by its id is never treated as yours, and nothing is uploaded until a **Download** has said which lodges are yours. This site and the central server must both be on 2.5; syncing pauses while they differ. The database change only replaces an index and needs no maintenance window.
