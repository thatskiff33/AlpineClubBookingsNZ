- **The admin search box now finds every admin page you can open (#4797).**
  Pressing Ctrl + K (or ⌘ + K), or clicking **Search…** at the top of the admin
  sidebar, used to find only the pages the sidebar links. It now finds forty
  more, among them Chores, Lockers, Seasons, Rooms & Beds, Email Messages,
  Notification Rules, Membership Types, Site Style, the booking policy pages
  and the Stripe, Google, Xero and backup setup wizards. Results are grouped by
  area (Bookings, Finance, Lodge and so on), and the old sidebar section names
  such as "Lodge Operations" still find their pages. A page that was merged
  into another, such as Family Suggestions, is listed under its old name and
  opens the tab that now holds it.

  It still lists only pages you are allowed to open, and hides a page whose
  module is turned off. A lodge page opened from it starts on the club's first
  lodge; choose another lodge on the page.
