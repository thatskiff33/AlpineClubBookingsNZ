- **The Xero sync error alert's one-an-hour limit now holds across copies of the app (#5407).**
  The alert still goes out at most once an hour across every kind of Xero error. It now keeps its own record of each alert it raised instead of reading the mail log, so an operator may notice five things. Two copies of the app hitting a Xero error at the same moment now send one alert, not two. An alert left in the mail log for the retry job, or withheld, counts as sent for the hour, as one already sent did. While the alert is switched off, or no officer is opted in to it, it is tried at most once a day, so after it is switched back on or an officer opts in, the next alert can wait up to a day after the last skipped attempt; a send that reached nobody because of a passing mail fault holds it for the hour only. The alerts other parts of the app send straight to officers about one record that needs action (a card payment Xero could not record, a credit-release payment, a group settlement hand-back, a membership cancellation that refunded nothing or stranded an invoice, a change paid twice, an unrecorded capture, a Xero success after a resolved failure) no longer count toward this hour, so one of them no longer holds back the next general sync error alert; most of them have a limit of their own. Just after this release, one extra alert can go out within the hour of an earlier one.
