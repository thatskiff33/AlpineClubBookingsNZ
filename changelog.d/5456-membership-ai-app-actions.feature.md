- **An officer with the Membership area can now work through a connected AI app (ALP-720).**
  Sixty-five actions cover the area's screens: finding and reading members and their family links, lodge access, family groups and their requests, applications and the request queues, club notices and the message board, committee roles, inductions and lockers. Any action that emails or notifies someone else, or changes another person's record, is marked destructive, so the app asks the officer to confirm it first and the call is logged as important. An officer without the area never sees them, a view-only grant reaches only the reads, and every action answers with named fields only: no dates of birth, addresses, dietary or medical details, access roles or sign-in details. Fresh-proof actions (merge, deletion, role changes, export), deciding requests and applications, anything that changes what a member owes, sign-in links, bulk email, membership settings and overriding an induction stay on the website. The privacy register's `ai-apps` row now says what an officer's connection sends.
