- **An officer with the Membership area can now work through a connected AI app (ALP-720).**
  Eighty-nine actions cover the area's screens: finding and reading members and their family links, lodge access, family groups and their requests, applications and the request queues, club notices and the message board, committee roles, inductions and lockers, and the Membership configuration: message-board, member-field, cancellation, nomination and lockout settings, membership types, subscription categories and induction checklists. Any action that emails or notifies someone else, changes another person's record or changes Membership configuration is marked destructive, so the app asks the officer to confirm it first and the call is logged as important. Where the website lets the officer choose whether a member is emailed, the app can pass the same choice. Changing the subscription-liability rule still needs Finance edit as well. Officer actions are offered only on a connection the officer approved on the screen that says the app can act as an officer; an officer without the area never sees them, a view-only grant reaches only the reads, and every action answers with named fields only: no dates of birth, addresses, dietary or medical details, access roles or sign-in details. Fresh-proof actions (merge, deletion, role changes, export), deciding requests and applications, anything that changes what a member owes, sign-in links, bulk email and overriding an induction stay on the website. The privacy register's `ai-apps` row says what an officer's connection sends.
