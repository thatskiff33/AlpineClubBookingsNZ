## Who

officer

## What to do

Only if a group organiser's whole-group card refund was closed on Stuck States
as "Paid another way" since v0.14.0 (17 Nov 2026): open each of that group's
joiners' invoices in Xero. The nightly Xero check may have raised a card refund
note on a joiner's invoice beside the credit note an officer made by hand,
which credits that money twice. Void whichever of the two is not wanted.

## How to check it worked

Each joiner's invoice carries one credit note for the refunded money, or the
club closed no such refund.
