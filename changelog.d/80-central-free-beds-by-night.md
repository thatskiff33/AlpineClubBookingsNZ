- **Alpine Central Server: free beds night by night ([triyder/AlpineClubBookingsNZ#79](https://github.com/triyder/AlpineClubBookingsNZ/issues/79)).** When the central server asks how many beds are free at your own lodge, this site now answers the free beds on each night it asks about (up to 30 nights) as well as the fewest of them, so the server's new bed planner can show a lodge's free beds night by night. The numbers are the member calendar's own (a night the whole lodge is booked or held reads as full). Nothing else is shared: no guest, member, booking or hold. A central server that does not use the new list is unaffected.
