- **Charging a saved card or settling a group by card now says when the amount is too small.** An amount above $0 but under 50 hundredths of the club's currency used to fail with a generic error. Before it failed, a saved-card charge had already claimed the booking and sent admins a payment-failure alert. Charging a booking's saved card now answers that no card was charged because the amount is below the smallest card charge, and to settle it another way. It does this before claiming the booking, recording a charge attempt or calling Stripe, and sends no failure alert. Settling an organiser-pays group by card gives the organiser the same "too small to pay by card" message as **Pay**, before any joiner's booking is confirmed or Stripe is called. Amounts of 50 and above are unchanged.
