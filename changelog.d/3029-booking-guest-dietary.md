- **Each booking now carries its guests' dietary and allergy information for
  that stay, seen only by booking officers and the hut leader running it
  (#3029).** When the club collects **Dietary/allergy information** (Admin →
  Member Fields), a member added to a booking brings the note from their
  profile, copied once. From then on the booking's copy belongs to that stay: a
  later profile change does not alter it, and correcting it for one trip does
  not change the profile. Non-member guests can have a note too.

  Booking officers see a **Dietary/allergy information** card on the booking
  page (bookings view), and can edit each guest's note there (bookings edit).
  Saving a note does not reprice the booking, email anybody or touch Xero, and
  the audit log records only that it changed. The hut leader running the stay —
  on their own account or unlocked with their PIN on the lodge kiosk — sees each
  present guest's note on the kiosk day list; so does a full admin. The member
  who made the booking, the other guests, the plain lodge screen, the lobby
  wall, the chore roster, the member roster, reports and exports, emails and
  Xero never receive it. A member's own "Download My Data" export includes the
  notes held on their own guest rows.

  Turning the field off hides every booking note without deleting any; turning
  it on does not fill in bookings made while it was off. Moving dates, removing
  a guest, a waitlist promotion and arrival or departure keep each note as it
  is, an approval that rebuilds a held party never moves one person's note onto
  somebody else, and copying a booking starts the notes afresh from current
  profiles. Backups now hold these booking notes as well as the profile values.
