- **A price increase's retry no longer interrupts a member who is confirming a later card request with their bank (#4755).** When an increase's card request failed to be created and was waiting on a retry, and the member was meanwhile on their bank's 3D Secure page for a later request, the retry replaced that request: the bank page failed, or a payment the member had just finished was refunded and asked for again. The retry now waits, checking every 15 minutes, so the member can finish; the earlier increase is then asked for on its own. A bank page left open for more than an hour counts as abandoned, and the retry replaces it with one request for both amounts, as before.
