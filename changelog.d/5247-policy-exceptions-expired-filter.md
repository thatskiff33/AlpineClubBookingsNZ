- **Policy Exceptions has an Expired filter, and keeps its filter and page in the address (#5247).**
  On the Booking Requests page, the Policy Exceptions tab now offers an
  Expired filter that lists the requests closed because their bed hold ran out
  before anyone decided, newest first, with the same pager as the other
  filters. Officers no longer have to look for them under All. The chosen
  filter and page are now kept in the web address, as on the other tabs, so a
  reload or a shared link opens the same filter and page. An unknown filter in
  the address opens Requested. Moving to another tab starts that tab on its own
  default filter.
