- **Hut-leader assignments now cover only the nights the leader stays (#3817).**
  The nightly automatic assignment ends on the last night slept, never the
  check-out day, and gives a split stay one assignment per run of nights. An
  officer can no longer assign a night the member is not staying at that lodge:
  the page names the night and offers to change the last night to the last one
  stayed (a custodian holding a bed is exempt — the bed is their stay). A leader
  can now sign in from the day before their first night until midnight on the
  day they leave. The nightly job also now starts from today rather than
  yesterday.
