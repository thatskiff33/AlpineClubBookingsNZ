- **The Alpine Central Server's public lodge search can now show your lodge's free beds ([triyder/AlpineClubBookingsNZ#62](https://github.com/triyder/AlpineClubBookingsNZ/issues/62)).** When a non-member searches the central server for dates and a party size, it asks this site how many beds are free at your own lodge and is told the free beds for the stay, as the member calendar counts them (night by night since [#79](https://github.com/triyder/AlpineClubBookingsNZ/issues/79)). No guest, member or booking detail is ever sent. Each question is signed with the message-board push secret, so it needs the **Message board** module on and an `https://` site address; and it answers nothing while **Other lodges** sharing is off. A new **Central lodge** card on each lodge's hub (**Admin → Lodges → Configure**) links the lodge to its entry on the central server by the server's permanent lodge id, so a rename there does not break it. Every club saves that link, a club with one lodge included: until it is saved the lodge answers nothing and the search shows its free beds as unknown. A club with one lodge that owns one central lodge finds it already chosen when it clicks **Edit**, and **Save** links it. The database change only adds columns and needs no maintenance window. Changing the link writes one audit record, `LODGE_CENTRAL_LINK_UPDATED`, in the `admin` category like every other lodge setting; that is one more audit site readable with **support: view** (two central lodge ids and the administrator, no member data).
