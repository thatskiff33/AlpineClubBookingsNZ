- **Removing a credit note's allocation in Xero now lowers what a payment looks to have refunded (#4447).**
  Deleting an allocation in Xero, while leaving the credit note itself, used to leave that amount counted as already refunded. The next update from Xero drops it, and an older update that arrives later does not put it back, so a later cancellation is not told there is less left to refund than there is.

  No money moves on its own.
