- **A stalled organiser waiver can no longer void the invoice after an officer took the job over.** When a group organiser's unpaid extra is waived in full, the app voids its Xero invoice. If that Xero job stalled and an officer reset it and resolved it by hand, the stalled run could still wake and void the invoice. It now checks the job is still its own just before the void; if not, it voids nothing, records an audit entry, and, when the job was resolved by hand, emails the administrators once to say the run stopped (#4701).
