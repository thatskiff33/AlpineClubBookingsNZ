- **A lodge deleted on the Alpine Central Server is now hidden here too, and kept for the bookings that name it ([triyder/AlpineClubBookingsNZ#58](https://github.com/triyder/AlpineClubBookingsNZ/issues/58)).** The central server now marks a deleted lodge rather than removing it, and tells every club about it (server **API 2.4**), however long since the club last downloaded. On the next download the lodge leaves the Other lodges list, the booking-request form and the visiting-club rate picker. Every booking and booking request that already names it keeps it and shows it as before, and the **Download** message names it. A lodge marked **Not found on the Alpine Central Server** (for example a duplicate left by an earlier rename) now has a **Hide** button, for an administrator with lodge edit rights, after a confirmation; every hide is audited. Lodges the central server still lists cannot be hidden here, and hiding is not undone. A hidden lodge keeps its name without blocking a live lodge of the same name: two lodges may share a name. This site and the central server must both be on 2.4; syncing pauses while they differ. The database change adds a column and makes lodge names unique among visible lodges only; it needs no maintenance window.
