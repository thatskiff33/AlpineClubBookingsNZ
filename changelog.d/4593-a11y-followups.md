- **Payment choices, inactive rows and error messages are clearer to read and
  hear (#4563).** On admin Book, the organiser's group settlement and the group
  join page, the chosen payment option (Card or Internet Banking) was shown by
  colour alone. It now shows a check mark and the word "Selected", and a screen
  reader announces which option is chosen, as the member booking screen already
  did.

  Inactive chores, committee roles and assignments, booking policies and
  periods, family members and fully covered hut-leader candidates are no longer
  greyed out to the point of being hard to read. They keep their normal text
  and say "Inactive" or "Archived"; inactive cards also have a dashed border.

  In light mode, error messages use a slightly darker red, so they stay
  readable when they appear on a grey panel. On the committee page, the row
  edit and remove buttons now tell a screen reader which role or member they
  act on.
