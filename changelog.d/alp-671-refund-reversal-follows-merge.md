- **A refund debt withdrawn after the booking's owner was merged into another member now moves to the surviving member in the member-money journal (#5191).**
  When an officer keeps a late card capture or dismisses a card refund shortfall's part to check, and the booking's owner has since been merged into another member, the journal used to leave the withdrawn amount on the merged-away member, which the nightly census reported as a merged-debtor balance. The reversal now carries that amount to the surviving member, the way a reversed receipt already did, and carries the debt itself too when it was posted on the merged-away member after the merge. An appeal's approval released because its card refund could not be sent now locks the booking before the request, as the approval does, so it reads the same owner. No member is paid or charged differently; rows posted before this change are not corrected.
