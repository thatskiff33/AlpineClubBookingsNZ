- **Billing and booking now ask one rule whether a member owes a subscription, and nothing changes until your club chooses it (#5190).**
  Until now, billing (who is invoiced) and booking (who must pay before booking) could disagree for two kinds of member: a child on an always-required membership type, whom billing invoiced but booking let through, and a member with a saved "Not required" record on a type that owes, whom booking let through but billing still invoiced. A club can now choose one rule for both: whether an exempt age tier exempts a member on an always-required type, and whether the age on the first or the last day of the season decides the tier. Once chosen, a saved "Not required" record no longer exempts anyone on its own, and any untouched "Not required" record for a member who now owes is updated.
  Every club arrives on **Not chosen**, which keeps both answers exactly as they were. Choosing it needs both Membership edit and Finance edit (owner decision 23 Nov 2026). The settings screen for the choice comes in a later release; until then it can be made through the lockout settings API or a configuration import (full admin), and `GET /api/admin/membership-lockout-settings/liability-impact` lists who a proposed rule would start or stop charging. Read it before choosing. A chosen rule cannot be set back to Not chosen. If you deploy blue-green, do not choose a rule until the old colour has drained.
  One change applies to every club straight away: a member covered by a no-invoice fee is now treated as paid for booking, since they were never sent an invoice to pay. The admin members list, its export and the subscriptions list now show who billing invoices until your club chooses a rule, and the chosen rule after that. So, while Not chosen, a child on an always-required membership type now counts as owing there, as billing invoices them, even though booking still lets them book. Organisation and school accounts on an always-required type keep showing as owing.
- **Rows marked "Not required" that billing treats as owing now say so (#5190, owner decision 22 Nov 2026).**
  On the admin members list and the subscriptions list, an active member who has a saved "Not required" record for the season, but whom billing treats as owing, shows as **Not invoiced**, as billing sees them, with the note "Marked not required, but billing treats this member as owing"; the "Not invoiced" filters and counts include them. The members export shows the same status and carries the note in a new last column, **Subscription Note** (empty for every other row), so no existing column moves. After upgrading, these are the rows to check: typically a child on an always-required membership type, or a member on an age-based type whose age tier now owes. If the member should not pay, move them to a membership type whose subscription is not required; if they should pay, leave them, and the next billing run bills them as their fee says (a no-invoice fee, or a family fee billed through another member, sends them no invoice of their own). For the current season, the note disappears once your club chooses its rule, because the member then either stops owing or their record is updated to "Not invoiced"; a record for another season keeps its note. A record a no-invoice fee already covers, or one already invoiced, paid or marked paid, never gets the note.
