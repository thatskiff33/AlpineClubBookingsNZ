- **A group organiser's card refund closed as "Paid another way" now credits Xero by itself (#5267).**
  When a refund back to the card a group organiser paid with fails for good, and the treasurer closes it on Stuck States as "Paid another way" because the organiser was paid back by bank transfer, the app now raises the Xero refund credit note itself. A group the organiser paid for by card has one Xero invoice per joiner, so the note goes on each joiner's invoice, worded as a bank transfer, for exactly that joiner's money - the same note any other "Paid another way" close raises. An old refund of the whole group raises one note per joiner, and the notes add up to what the bank paid back. Officers are no longer emailed to credit the group's invoice by hand. A note Xero refuses is listed in Xero Operations to retry, and a retry never raises a second one. A joiner whose booking has no Xero invoice gets no note, as with any other close; the dialog says so before you close it.

  This applies to closes made from this release on. If an old whole-group card refund was closed as "Paid another way" since v0.14.0 (14 Nov 2026), check that group's joiners in Xero: the nightly Xero check may have raised a card refund note on a joiner's invoice beside the credit note an officer made by hand, which credits that money twice. Void whichever is not wanted.
