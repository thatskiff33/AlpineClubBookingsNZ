- When nights or guests change on a booking that is already under way, the
  adjustment is now worked out from what each night was actually sold for, as
  recorded on the booking, and from nothing else. Nights the member keeps hold
  their stored price exactly; nights the change newly buys are priced under the
  club's current rates and group discount as before. Where the booking's own
  price history is incomplete or does not add up, the change is no longer priced
  from today's rate list, an average or an even split - the club is asked to
  confirm the amount instead, and no figure is shown or written in the meantime.
- Taking a guest off a booking now returns that guest's own recorded price. It
  used to be worked out by re-pricing everybody who was staying, so a rate rise
  on somebody else's stay could change what the departing guest was refunded.
