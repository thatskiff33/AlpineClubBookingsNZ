- **The member lodge roster now shows nights the whole lodge is privately
  booked** (#3474). In clubs that run the roster, nights under a whole-lodge
  hold appear on **Who's at the lodge** as one "Reserved for a private booking"
  row, without naming or counting the party: the holding booking is not listed
  as a group or as people. The booking calendar still shows those nights as
  full; before this change a member could tell a held night from a genuinely
  full one by comparing the calendar with the roster's head count, so the
  roster now says it outright, as it already does for custodians. Clubs that
  do not run the roster are unchanged. See
  [Who's at the lodge](../docs/user-guide/whos-at-the-lodge.md).
