- **Officers can add an existing booking to a work party** (fork #119, fork #129).
  For a member who forgot to tick "I am attending a working bee", a Booking
  Officer or Full Admin opens the booking and clicks **Add to work party** in
  its admin tools. They choose the work party and see the new total and the
  money coming back before confirming. The booking is repriced exactly as if
  the box had been ticked: an unpaid booking simply owes less, and on a paid
  booking everything paid above the new total comes back in full, with no
  change fee. The officer chooses how, as on an ordinary booking change: club
  credit (the default) or a refund, to the card for a card-paid booking or by
  bank transfer for one paid by Internet Banking (the treasurer's task in
  **Money to settle**). A booking paid wholly with account credit gets its
  credit back. A joiner's booking that the group organiser paid for goes back
  to the organiser, as with any change to it. An officer can never add their
  own booking or a family member's, nor one whose group they or their family
  organise and pay for, paid yet or not, even when no money comes back; another
  officer must. A booking priced by a quote can't be added, nor one whose card
  payment is disputed where money would come back. The change
  goes through the normal booking change, so the history, ledger, Xero and any
  refund are recorded and made like any other.
