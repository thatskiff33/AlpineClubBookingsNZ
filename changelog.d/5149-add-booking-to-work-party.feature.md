- **Officers can add an existing booking to a work party** (fork #119). For a
  member who forgot to tick "I am attending a working bee", a Booking Officer
  or Full Admin opens the booking and clicks **Add to work party** in its admin
  tools. They choose the work party and see the new total and the credit
  before confirming. The booking is repriced exactly as if the box had been
  ticked: an unpaid booking simply owes less, and on a paid booking everything
  paid above the new total goes back as account credit, in full, with no change
  fee. Nothing is ever refunded: a booking its group organiser paid can't be
  added, and neither can a stay that has already started or one priced by a
  quote. The change goes through the normal booking change, so the history,
  ledger and Xero record it like any other.
