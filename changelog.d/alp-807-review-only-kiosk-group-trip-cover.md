- **The lodge kiosk shows adult cover from another booking in a Group Trip at
  lodges whose adult cover rule sends bookings to a Booking Officer, not only at
  lodges where it stops the booking (#5239 follow-up).** A saved-card
  payment, a group settlement or its reversal, a member merge or an adult's
  membership changing now queues the same re-check at both settings, so the
  kiosk can show the real figures once that re-check has run. At the review
  setting the re-check raises no urgent incident and sends no email; it
  refreshes the booking's recorded review, so a booking whose cover has changed
  waits for a Booking Officer's review as it would after any other edit.

  A card checked before this release at a review-setting lodge still says
  *needs re-checking* until its booking is next checked.
