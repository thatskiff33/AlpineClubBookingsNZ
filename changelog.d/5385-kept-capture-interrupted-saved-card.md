- **A saved-card payment that arrives after the booking was paid another way is now always reported (#5240).**
  If a saved-card charge went through but the app stopped before marking the booking paid, and the booking was then paid another way (account credit, a $0 total, or the group organiser), the card payment that arrived later used to be kept with nobody told. The administrators now get the same "Kept card payment" alert as for any other card payment kept on a paid booking, at most once a day per payment. If the card payment is still not matched to its booking, after an hour the payments task sends that alert itself, giving the same reason the payment's own arrival would have given. It waits instead when another payment (card, cash or bank) already settled the booking, for the card payment's own arrival, which refunds it as a duplicate, so nobody is told a payment was kept that is then refunded. It also waits while a cancellation or refund in progress holds the money, checking again each hour. If that arrival has not come a week later, the administrators get a "Kept card payment" alert saying the saved-card payment was never settled. A card payment alerted this way can still be refunded automatically if Stripe later sends it again. On a booking that is not paid, a card payment still unmatched after a week is written to the error log for someone to check by hand.
  When a duplicate card payment is refunded by the background retry rather than at the first attempt, the booking history now names the payment that settled the booking, as it already did for a refund made straight away. No money moves differently.
