- **A review completed after a paid booking was cancelled no longer refunds
  its share on top of what the cancellation returned** (#3835). When a booking
  paid by card or by internet banking (with or without account credit) was
  cancelled before an open financial review was completed, the cancellation
  returned its tier of the full price - to the card, or as account credit for
  internet banking - and the review then refunded its whole share as well, to
  the card or as a bank-transfer hand-back. The review now gives back only what
  is still owed: what the cancellation would have returned had the share come
  back first, less what it actually returned, and never less than nothing. Two
  reviews of one booking share that between them. A $200 booking with a $50
  share returns $200 in total at a 100% tier, $105 at 50% with a $20 fee (was
  $130) and $50 at 0%, whichever came first and whichever way it was paid. The
  card refund or hand-back, the booking event, the booking ledger and the Xero
  leg all follow the netted figure; a share the cancellation had already
  returned completes with nothing sent, and the officer is told so. Before
  completing, the settle dialog shows what is still owed - "Only $25.00 of the
  $50.00 share is still owed after the booking's cancellation - hand back
  $25.00" - so a bank transfer is made for the right amount.
  What is still owed goes back the way it was paid: the part that came out of
  the member's account credit is given back as credit, and only the rest goes
  to the card or by bank transfer, so the card is never asked for more than it
  took.
  Where the current cancellation policy no longer reproduces what the
  cancellation returned, the review is refused and stays open for the officer
  to settle by hand. This applies the rule #3791 introduced for credit-paid
  bookings.
