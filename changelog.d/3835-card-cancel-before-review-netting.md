- **A review completed after a card-paid booking was cancelled no longer
  refunds its share on top of the cancellation's refund** (#3835). When a
  booking paid by card, or by credit plus card, was cancelled before an open
  financial review was completed, the cancellation refunded by its tier on the
  full price and the review then refunded its whole share as well. The review
  now gives back only what is still owed: what the cancellation would have
  returned had the share come back first, less what it actually returned, and
  never less than nothing. Two reviews of one booking share that between them.
  A $200 card-paid booking with a $50 share returns $200 in total at a 100%
  tier, $105 at 50% with a $20 fee (was $130) and $50 at 0%, whichever came
  first. The card refund, the booking event, the booking ledger and the Xero
  leg all follow the netted figure; a share the cancellation had already
  returned completes with nothing sent, and the officer is told so. Where the
  current cancellation policy no longer reproduces what the cancellation
  refunded, the review is refused and stays open for the officer to settle by
  hand. This applies the rule #3791 introduced for credit-paid bookings.
