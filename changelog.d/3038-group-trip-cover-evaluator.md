- **A qualifying adult travelling on a related booking can now provide the
  required adult cover, where a club has switched that on (#3038).** A real
  travelling group is often several separate bookings, and the adult who is
  actually with everyone may be on a different one. Where a club has ticked
  **"Another booking in the same Group Trip"** on the **Adult-member hosting**
  card, the rule now looks at those sibling bookings: a booking short of adult
  cover is treated as covered for any night on which a qualifying adult member is
  genuinely staying at the same lodge on another live booking in the same Group
  Trip.

  It is decided night by night, not trip by trip. An adult staying only the
  Friday covers only the Friday, and an adult at a different lodge, on a
  cancelled booking, or on a booking that is not yet confirmed covers nothing.
  Members who join with a login and people who join through the public link are
  treated exactly the same way.

  Nothing about the other booking moves. The covering adult does not take a bed
  on the booking they are covering, does not appear on its guest list, does not
  change its price, and takes on no responsibility for its payment or its
  cancellation. Their own booking's guests remain their own booking's business.
  And nobody is told who supplied the cover: a member short of cover is told what
  would satisfy the rule, never which booking or which person is providing it.

  Closing a Group Trip, or cancelling the trip itself, does not withdraw cover.
  Those decide whether new bookings may still join; the people already booked are
  still travelling, and their own bookings are what say whether they are coming.

  **Clubs that leave the box unticked see no change of any kind** — the same
  bookings are compliant, the same bookings go to a Booking Officer, the wording
  is unchanged to the letter, and the application does not even look for related
  bookings.

  This is the second of four parts. Keeping sibling bookings correct after one of
  them is changed, and the kiosk display of the linkage, arrive with the rest of
  the same piece of work.
