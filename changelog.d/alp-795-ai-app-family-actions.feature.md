- **A connected AI app can now help with the member's family group, partner
  link and guest lookups (ALP-795).** With **AI app connections (MCP)**
  switched on, a member's AI app can list their family group invitations,
  invite an adult member, ask for a new family group, ask to join one, ask to
  add a child or an adult without a login, ask to remove someone, and confirm
  the details of a family member without a login. It can read their partner
  link, ask a partner with a login to confirm, withdraw or end a link, read
  whose memberships they may ask to cancel, and look up another member to add
  as a booking guest, by name where the club has name search switched on, or
  by exact email address. Changes still need a connection the member approved
  for **View and change**, and each one is recorded in the audit log.

  A guest lookup sends the app only what the website's own guest picker shows
  (a member's id, name and age group), never contact details or dates of
  birth, and every lookup is still limited and recorded against the member.
  Accepting a family invitation, confirming a partnership, declaring a
  family member without a login as a partner, and asking for or confirming a
  membership cancellation stay on the website. If you named what an AI app
  receives in your privacy notice, the recipient register's `ai-apps` row now
  lists the new information.
