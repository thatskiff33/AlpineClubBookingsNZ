- **A booking's total no longer disagrees with its own nights after an officer
  settles a financial review (#3219).** When a change to a booking cannot be
  priced from what the club has on record, the booking is parked and its total is
  deliberately frozen — nobody yet knows what the change was worth. An officer
  then settles the review on the payments screen and, where the booking has
  nights with no stored price, records what those nights sold for.

  Until now the freeze was never lifted. The guest's stay was brought up to date
  and the booking's headline total was not, so from that moment the figure at the
  top of the booking and the figures on its nights disagreed permanently, by
  exactly the amount that had been settled, with nothing reconciling them and no
  indication which was right. An officer quoting a member from the booking page,
  a member looking at their own booking, and any report that adds up booking
  totals could each be given a different answer.

  Recording the night prices now brings the booking's total and its final price
  back into line with its guests, in the same save. Two details are worth knowing:
  it happens on **No adjustment** just as it does on **Record the adjustment** —
  a review closed with nothing owed still leaves the booking's figures needing to
  agree — and the new total is worked out from the booking's guests rather than
  from the amount that was settled, so it can move by more or less than the
  settlement did. That happens when the change that parked the booking also took
  a guest off it, for instance.

  Nothing changes for a settle where the boxes are left blank, which is still a
  valid answer: that booking's total stays exactly where it was. If somebody
  edits the booking while the settle box is open, the save is refused and nothing
  is written, so two people working at once cannot leave the booking holding half
  an answer.
