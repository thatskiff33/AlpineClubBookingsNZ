- **Admin and member actions that queue behind a long booking job now wait their turn instead of failing, and say "busy, try again" when they cannot (follow-up to #4933).** About 140 actions that take the booking system's global lock (cancelling, confirming payments, moving beds, approving requests, refunds and similar) used to give up after 5 seconds when an officer was running a long job such as assigning beds across a busy week, and most then showed a generic error. They now wait up to 30 seconds, the same allowance #4933 gave booking create. If one still cannot get in, the person sees that the system is busy and to try again shortly; where an earlier step of a multi-step action may already have gone through, they are asked to check the current status first. Nightly jobs and payment notifications keep retrying on their own schedule. Three writers deliberately keep a different limit, each explained in `docs/audits/lock1-transaction-budgets.md`. No settings change is needed.
