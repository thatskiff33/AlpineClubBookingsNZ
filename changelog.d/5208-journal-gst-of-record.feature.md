- **New booking invoices now carry the club's own GST figure on every line, so Xero's GST matches the club's records to the cent (#5208).** The club's records work out a booking's GST once, on the whole stay. Xero used to work it out line by line, which could leave the two a cent or two apart on a booking. A new booking invoice for a GST-registered club now tells Xero the GST on each line, shared out from the club's own figure, so the two always agree. Prices, invoice totals and anything already sent to Xero do not change.

  After every booking invoice it raises or updates, the app checks that Xero kept the GST it was sent. If Xero changed it, the app stops sending its own GST figures, Xero works the GST out itself on new invoices, and a "GST overrides paused" item appears on the Finance dashboard, in the Xero health check and on a new "GST overrides" card on the Xero page. A finance editor checks the listed invoices in Xero, ticks that they have, writes what they found and resumes. Nothing is re-sent.

  Change-fee invoices raised after the first one, edit credit notes and group settlement invoices still let Xero work out the GST line by line. How GST is rounded is the club's to confirm with its accountant.
