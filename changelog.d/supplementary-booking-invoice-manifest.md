- **Supplementary invoices and updated booking invoices are now checked against
  the booking's ledger too.** When a booking change raises a supplementary
  invoice in Xero, or an issued booking invoice is updated, the app records
  whether what was sent matches what the ledger says that change charged, or
  why it does not. What is sent to Xero, and when, is unchanged. Clubs need to
  do nothing.
