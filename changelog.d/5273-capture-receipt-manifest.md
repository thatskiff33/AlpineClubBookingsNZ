- **Card payments recorded in Xero are now noted against the card capture they came from (#4525, Ledger M13).**
  Nothing sent to Xero changes. When the app records a Stripe payment on a Xero invoice (the booking's own invoice, a booking change's invoice or receipt, a kept late payment, or an officer's repair of a partly failed invoice), it now also writes a permanent internal note of which card capture those cents came from, under the payment's own key. A payment for more than its capture has left, or one whose capture the app's ledger has no record of, is noted as a mismatch instead. These notes are groundwork for the ledger sending Xero's documents itself later; a failure to write one is logged and never stops the payment.
