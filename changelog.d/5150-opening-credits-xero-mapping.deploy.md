## Who

owner

## What to do

Only if the club is GST-registered and imports opening member credit that is
already recorded in Xero: a Finance Admin or Full Admin maps **Exempt** to an
active 0% Xero tax rate (Admin → Xero → Setup), then maps **Opening Credits** to
a liability account that no other mapping uses.

If that credit was never recorded in Xero, leave Opening Credits unset. If you
map it while the club is not registered, map Exempt before any scheduled GST
registration begins.

## How to check it worked

The Xero health check shows no Opening Credits line, or the club imports no
opening credit.
