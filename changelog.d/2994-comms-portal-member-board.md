- **Members can now write to a club message board (#2994).** A new
  **Message board** page lets any member post something the club would want to
  know — a road report, a reminder, an offer of a lift — and read what others
  have written. The newest few posts also appear on the dashboard.

  Everything posted stays **inside your own club**. A "Share with all clubs"
  tickbox sits under the writing box, switched off and not yet usable, so
  members can see the option is coming; until it works there is nothing that
  leaves the club.

  Posts are plain text, up to 4000 characters, limited to ten an hour per
  member so one person cannot fill the board. A member's name comes from their
  own record, so nobody can post under someone else's. Members cannot edit or
  delete their own posts yet — a club administrator will be able to hide or
  remove any post in the next release.

  The whole feature is behind the **Message board** module, which is off by
  default. Existing installs see no change until an administrator switches it on
  at **Admin → Setup & Configuration → Modules**.
