- **A late booking-change payment refunded by bank is recorded once in Xero (#4381).**
  When a treasurer closes the refund of a late card payment for a booking change as "Paid another way", and the booking's Xero invoice already bills that change, the app now records the payment against that invoice and credits the bank refund against it. It no longer raises a second invoice that counted the money as income twice and left the original invoice owing. Where the club's credit-release sweep holds the whole payment, no bank refund note is raised in Xero until #4380 lands. Closes made before this change are not repaired automatically.
