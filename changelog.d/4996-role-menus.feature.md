- **One admin menu per role.** The admin sidebar's switcher now offers **Admin**, **Bookings**, **Membership**, **Content**, **Finance** and **Treasurer** menus, one for each role a person holds, worked out from that role's permission areas so club-made roles follow the same rule. A Full Admin sees **Admin** only, plus any other role they hold; other roles get no full-menu option. **Treasurer** is the **Finance** menu plus Subscriptions, Integrations and the refund and unpaid-stay queues. The sidebar no longer repeats the Member Dashboard and Admin Dashboard links from the top bar, and staff pages (`/admin` and `/finance`) leave Book and My Bookings out of the top bar. **Who's at the lodge** has left the top bar for everyone and is now a button under **Open Kiosk** on the dashboard's Lodge Kiosk card, and an Admin account that is not also a club member no longer sees **My Bookings**. The menus are display only — page access is unchanged.
