- **More admin pages now move to a failed action's explanation, and do it again when the same failure repeats (#4975).** On the booking desk's **Book** page, **Family Groups**, **Mountain Conditions**, **AI Assistant**, **Backups**, **Age Tier Settings**, **Seasons**, the refund and credit review queue, and a member's committee and lodge-access cards, a failed save, review or other action now takes keyboard and screen-reader focus to its message. Pressing the same button again and getting the same refusal announces it again, where before a word-for-word repeat could pass unnoticed.

  A page that simply fails to load its data still shows the message in place without moving focus, and toasts and messages inside open dialogs are unchanged. After a save that closes the editor on **Age Tier Settings** or **Seasons**, a season deletion, or a committee assignment change, the page now keeps the edited section in view. Committee confirmations also stay visible while the member record refreshes.
