- **The member-money ledger now has a real-database test for a card refund shortfall paid back by bank when the lost card refund fails late (#4488).**
  It follows the worked example the refund fix in #5313 proved: a $300 booking paid $200 by card and $100 by internet banking, cancelled, the card refund lost and closed "Paid another way", the $100 shortfall paid back, then the lost refund recorded late and failed. The ledger's records of who is owed what must show each refund paid once, the late refund cancelled out exactly once, nothing owed again, and no room for a second card refund. Test and documentation only; no behaviour changes for clubs.
