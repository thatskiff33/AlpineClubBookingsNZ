- **The Xero Account Mappings step no longer keeps reloading when its load fails (#5203).** On the Xero setup screen, the Account mapping step used to request the mappings again and again when they could not be read, so the error flickered in and out. The step now tries once each time the admin arrives at it; a failure stays shown until the admin presses **Refresh Xero reference data**, leaves the step and comes back, or reconnects Xero. The contact-group list on the Import and finish step already behaved this way and now shares the same rule.
