- **The four ways of editing a booking now load the booking the same way
  (#4925).** The full edit screen, a date change, removing a guest and a guest
  accepting an invitation each loaded the booking with their own hand-written
  list of what to read, and the shared refund, credit and charge code trusted
  that every list matched. They now share one list, so a future change to the
  shared money code cannot quietly leave one of them short.

  Nothing a member, officer or treasurer sees has changed: refunds, account
  credit, extra charges and booking statuses come out exactly as before.
