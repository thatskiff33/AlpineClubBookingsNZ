- **The "Confirm member details" pop-up now has a Log out button.** The pop-up
  still cannot be closed any other way, but a member who cannot finish it — on
  a shared computer, or when their details fail to load — can now sign out
  instead of closing the tab and leaving their details on screen. Logging out
  does not mark the details confirmed; the pop-up returns at the next sign-in
  (#4953).
