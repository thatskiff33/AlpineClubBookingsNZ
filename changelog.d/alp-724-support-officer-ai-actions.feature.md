- **Officers with Support & System, or Admin Overview, can now use a connected AI app for that area's admin work (ALP-724).**
  50 new actions cover the health report, Stuck States, the officer work-queue counts (Admin Overview), the setup checklist and provider tests, backup status and run-now, the modules, the club's currency and locale, the sign-in rules and access roles (read only), privacy retention, the audit log, problem reports, booking message wording, email templates, the club's email settings, delivery policies, officers' email alerts, stopped addresses, failed emails, and the AI help assistant's and AI Diagnostics' spend. Each is offered only to an officer who holds the area (view for reads, edit for changes), on a connection they approved on the officer screen, and the route still checks the officer's permissions as for the browser. Any change that reaches members or another person's record, or changes a club-wide setting, is marked destructive, so the app asks the officer first; only setup ticks, provider tests and starting a backup are not. A connection searches the audit log only in the categories the officer's areas may read, as AI Diagnostics does, and is never sent a problem report's screenshot, email or IP addresses from the audit log, or the lodge door code. The AI app connection settings, fresh-proof actions (backup restore and destination, configuration import, module switches, sign-in rules, access role changes), the Full Admin settings (currency change, time zone, environment safety), asking AI Diagnostics a question, configuration bundles and reissuing a failed email's link stay on the website. The email templates list no longer has to carry every template's wording: `GET /api/admin/email-templates?templateName=` answers one template whole. Nothing changes until a club switches on **Officers may connect an AI app**.
