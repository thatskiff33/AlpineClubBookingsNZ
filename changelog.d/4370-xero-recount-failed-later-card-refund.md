- **A card refund Stripe failed later no longer creeps back into a payment's refunded total (#4370).** When Stripe accepts a card refund and then fails it, the refund's credit note stays in Xero. Any later credit note on the same payment, such as a second refund, used to count the failed refund again, so the payment showed more refunded than the member actually got back.

  The recount now takes each failed refund off once. If an officer closed it "Paid another way", the Stripe reversal invoice already takes it off; if that reversal is voided or deleted in Xero, the failed refund counts again. An older failure that was never reversed comes off through the record of the failure, never more than the payment's refund notes cover above the money still standing. Payments with no failed refund are counted exactly as before, and nothing is called in Xero. An affected payment is corrected at its next credit-note event.
