- **Officers with Bookings & Beds can now use a connected AI app for that area's admin work (ALP-719).**
  93 new actions cover booking search and the officer tools on a booking, group, school and whole-lodge requests, change and exception requests, booking reviews, the waitlist, occupancy, promo codes, bed allocation, rooms and beds, seasons, booking policies and the area's settings. Each is offered only to an officer who holds the permission its route needs (Bookings & Beds view or edit), and the route still checks the officer's permissions as for the browser. The approval screen now tells an officer that the app can also read and change the club records their admin role allows, such as other members' bookings and details and the club's booking settings. Only a connection approved on that screen can use officer actions, and that one approval also covers officer areas added later. An officer who connected an app before this release connects it again to use them, and keeps their member actions meanwhile. Every change to club-wide configuration (seasons and hut rates, booking periods, minimum stays, policies, age groups, settings, rooms and beds, promo codes) is marked destructive, so the app asks the officer first. The nine steps that ask an officer to prove who they are again stay website-only, and so does saving the member-guest settings, which decides whether members' names can be browsed (owner decision D-18), and changing a guest's dietary or allergy needs, which an app is never shown. Withdrawing an extra payment request is left to the Finance actions. A connection receives only the fields each action names; the AI app guide and the privacy register now say which other members' information an officer's connection sends. Nothing changes until a club switches on **Officers may connect an AI app**.
