- **A new "Message board" switch appears on the Modules page, turned off
  (#2993).** This is the first piece of a member-written message board for the
  club. Nothing is visible to members yet and the switch does nothing on its
  own — the screens that use it arrive in the next releases.

  It is off by default on purpose, and it will stay off until an administrator
  turns it on. Once the board is complete, a member will be able to choose to
  share a post with every other club connected to the Alpine Central Server, so
  switching this on is a decision about publishing your members' writing beyond
  your own club rather than only a display preference. The card on the Modules
  page says so.

  The board itself will not need a central-server connection: posts stay inside
  your club unless a member deliberately ticks "share with all clubs".

  Existing installs upgrade with nothing to configure and no change to anything
  members or administrators currently see.
