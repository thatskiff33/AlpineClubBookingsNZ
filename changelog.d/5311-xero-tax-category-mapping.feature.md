- **Map the club's tax categories to your own Xero tax rates (#5209).**
  Once Xero is connected, a new **Tax categories** card on the Xero setup screen's account-mapping step and on the Xero page maps each of the club's four tax categories — Sales tax on income, Sales tax on expenses, Exempt and Zero rated — to a tax rate your Xero organisation provides, so no Xero tax code is built into the app. Sales tax on income is still set on the **GST / tax treatment** card and is shown read-only. Exempt is what a spent opening credit coded to an Opening Credits liability account will use; Sales tax on expenses and Zero rated are not used by any document yet. Only a Full Admin can change a mapping; other finance admins see it read-only. While the club is GST-registered, a line in a category that is not mapped is held back rather than sent with a guessed code, and the Xero health check warns when a mapped rate is removed or made inactive in Xero, or was mapped in a different Xero organisation. Nothing changes until you map a category: every document is sent exactly as before.
