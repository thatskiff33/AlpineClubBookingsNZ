- **Finance can now see overpayments and prepayments sitting in Xero (#5204).**
  When a member pays more than they owe, or pays before any invoice exists, Xero holds the money as an overpayment or prepayment on their contact. The new **Finance → Xero Unallocated Payments** page lists every one that still has a balance: its type, date and balance, the member it belongs to (or "No member linked"), and a link to the contact in Xero. The list refreshes nightly, and an officer with finance edit access can refresh it from Xero at any time; a refresh that cannot read Xero changes nothing and says so.
  The page is for looking only in this release. Allocate the money in Xero as before; turning it into member credit comes later. The database gains the tables that later work needs, unused for now; no existing data changes.
