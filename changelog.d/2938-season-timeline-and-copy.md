- **The pricing screens now show your seasons as a schedule, warn about nights
  no season covers, and can start a new season from an existing one (#2938).**
  Both **Fees → Hut Fees** and **Seasons** listed seasons in whatever order the
  database happened to return them, which could differ between one refresh and
  the next. They are now in date order, earliest first.

  If any nights fall between two seasons, the screen says so where they fall —
  "No season covers 1 Oct 2026 to 30 Nov 2026 — 61 nights" — with a count above
  the list. Those nights cannot be booked, because nothing prices them, and
  until now the usual way a club found out was a member's booking failing on
  the one night nothing covered. The screen only tells you: it never fills the
  gap in, charges zero, or borrows a neighbouring season's rates. Note that a
  deactivated season does not count as cover, so if a window sits in a reported
  gap, check its Active badge — that is usually the explanation.

  Seasons that abut correctly are not flagged. A season prices every night up to
  and including its end date, so a season starting 1 October runs straight on
  from one ending 30 September.

  **New season from this** on a season card opens a new season pre-filled with
  that season's type, its Active setting, its flat whole-lodge rate and every
  nightly rate. The season you copied from is never changed, you still give the
  new one its own name and dates, and a rate box the original left blank arrives
  blank rather than as $0.00 — so a copy cannot quietly start charging a
  membership type nothing. A rate deliberately set to $0.00 is carried across as
  $0.00.
